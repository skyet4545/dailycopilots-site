# Bible Copilot product-claim boundary candidate

## Outcome

Protect reader trust and qualified Pro conversion by making onboarding and paywall claims match current product evidence.

## Finding

The current product source and every prepared release branch repeated absolute or stale claims: every answer cites Scripture, every answer is rooted in the Bible, unlimited Scripture-cited answers, seminary-grade method, major views fairly presented, and five study modes. Current evidence supports six study modes, a reference-bearing answer design, and a local reference-navigation verifier. It does not prove every generated quotation, reference, interpretation, or theological comparison is correct or complete.

## Treatment

- Explain that AI answers are designed to include references readers can open and check.
- Invite readers to compare responses with Scripture in context.
- Say Theology mode aims to identify major Christian views and recommend reviewing important conclusions.
- Describe the Pro quantity benefit as unlimited AI study questions.
- Correct the onboarding count and list all six study modes.

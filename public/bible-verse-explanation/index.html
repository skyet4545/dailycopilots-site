How should I explain a Bible verse?

A responsible Bible verse explanation starts with context. Read the surrounding passage first, notice repeated words, compare translations, trace cross-references, and treat Greek or Hebrew claims carefully. Then separate what the text says from what it may mean and how it should be applied.

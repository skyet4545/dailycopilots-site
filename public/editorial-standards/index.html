Editorial standards

Study should stay close to the text.

Bible Copilot is a Bible-study companion, not a replacement for Scripture, a local church, trusted teachers, or careful theological judgment.

What our explanations aim to do

  • Begin with the passage and point readers back to the cited text.
  • Distinguish observation, historical context, interpretation, theology, application, and apologetics as different study lenses.
  • Acknowledge meaningful disagreement among faithful Christian readers where it is relevant.
  • Use clear language and mark uncertainty rather than presenting a disputed interpretation as settled fact.

What readers should not assume

Generated study help can be incomplete or mistaken. It should be checked against the biblical text and, where useful, reliable human scholarship and accountable pastoral guidance. Bible Copilot does not offer personal revelation, pastoral care, medical advice, legal advice, or a substitute for professional help.

How content is reviewed

Website articles are written and maintained as editorial study resources. We revise pages when a citation, explanation, claim, or product detail needs correction. Product capabilities, access limits, pricing, and trial terms are stated from the current product record and should be confirmed in the app or through Apple before purchase.

Questions or correction requests may be sent through the Bible Copilot about page. We welcome specific references, citations, and context.

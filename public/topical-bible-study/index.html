What is a topical Bible study?

A topical Bible study follows a theme across Scripture. It can be helpful for topics like prayer, anxiety, forgiveness, faith, hope, or wisdom — but only when each passage is read in its own context before being connected to the theme.

Use topics to gather Scripture, not flatten it

The safest topical studies let different passages speak with their own genre, audience, and purpose. Bible Copilot can help organize the study, but conclusions should still be tested against the biblical text and wise human guidance.

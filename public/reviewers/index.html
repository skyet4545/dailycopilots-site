Independent theological reviewer program

Bible Copilot is recruiting qualified reviewers to evaluate defined samples of AI-assisted Bible study material, methodology, and safety policies. Participation is disclosed precisely and does not imply endorsement of the entire product.

Who should apply

  • Faculty, graduates, or advanced students in biblical studies, theology, church history, apologetics, pastoral ministry, or relevant language disciplines
  • Pastors or ministry leaders with verifiable training and accountable ministry experience
  • Editors or researchers experienced in checking biblical citations, original-language claims, or historical assertions

What reviewers may assess

  • The AI Bible Study Accuracy Benchmark, including a defined prompt sample and scoring rubric
  • A defined group of passage explanations
  • Denominational-perspective and theological-humility policies
  • Greek, Hebrew, historical, or apologetics claims within a stated specialty
  • Correction procedures and AI safety language

Review materials

Review materials are shared with confirmed reviewers after scope and conflicts are documented. This protects the blinded review process while it is in progress.

Reviewers may assess textual grounding, citation integrity, context and reasoning, uncertainty and perspectives, usefulness and clarity, false-premise handling where applicable, and critical safety failures.

  1. Apply with your role, specialty, and conflicts.
  2. After scope is confirmed, score any assigned packet independently before reading other reviewer notes.
  3. Return the completed material with a conflict disclosure; results are not published until the reviewer-overlap process is complete.

Disclosure and independence

Any compensation, free access, affiliation, conflict, and material editorial relationship must be disclosed. Bible Copilot may publish a reviewer’s name only with permission and must state exactly what was reviewed. A blinded packet can reduce product-name bias, but it does not remove the funding conflict.

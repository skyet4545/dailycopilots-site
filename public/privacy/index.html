Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 16, 2026 · Last Updated: July 29, 2026

Bible Copilot ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the Bible Copilot mobile application (the "App") or website.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to provide our services:

  • Usage Data: Anonymous, aggregated data about how you use the App (e.g., features accessed, session duration). This data cannot be used to identify you personally.
  • Website Measurement: On the Gospel Briefing landing page, we record anonymous page views and App Store handoff clicks to understand aggregate interest. These records contain the event type, page path, referral host when supplied by your browser, and a short-lived session identifier. We do not add your name, email address, full referral URL, or IP address to these analytics records.
  • Device Information: Basic device type and operating system version for compatibility purposes.
  • Questions and Queries: Text you enter into Bible Copilot is sent to our AI provider (OpenAI) to generate responses. We do not permanently store your queries.
  • Subscription Data: If you subscribe to Bible Copilot Pro, Apple manages all payment processing. We receive only confirmation that a subscription is active — never your payment details.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require account creation or collect your name, email address, or personal identifiers.
  • We do not sell your data to third parties.
  • We do not display advertisements.
  • We do not track your location.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the App
  • Understand aggregate interest in the Gospel Briefing landing page and its App Store handoff
  • Process your Bible study queries via AI
  • Manage subscription status via Apple's In-App Purchase system
  • Fix bugs and improve performance

4. Third-Party Services

Bible Copilot uses the following third-party services:

  • OpenAI: Powers AI-generated Bible study responses. Queries are processed in accordance with OpenAI's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple In-App Purchases: Manages subscription billing. Governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase: Stores the limited anonymous App and website event records described above.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your saved verses, bookmarks, and journal entries are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to our servers. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect any data we do process.

6. Children's Privacy

Bible Copilot is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

Public methodology

Trust must be inspectable.

Bible Copilot is an AI-assisted study tool, not Scripture, a pastor, a church, or a theological authority. This page explains what the system is for, where it can fail, how claims should be checked, and how corrections and independent reviews are handled.

Current review status: Bible Copilot does not claim that every app answer or website article has been individually reviewed by a named biblical scholar. When qualified review occurs, the specific reviewer, scope, date, and material reviewed should be identified.
Benchmark in progress: The AI Bible Study Accuracy Benchmark 2026 has a preregistered 100-prompt methodology and reviewer process. Scores are not published yet because collection and blinded review are incomplete.

Scripture first

Explanations should keep the biblical passage and surrounding context visible. Generated language must never be represented as a biblical quotation or divine speech.

Checkable references

Users should be able to inspect cited passages. Historical, lexical, pricing, and product claims should use identifiable sources when they materially affect an answer.

Interpretive humility

Where major Christian traditions differ, the difference should be labeled. The system should not turn one contested interpretation into universal Christian agreement.

Visible limitations

AI can fabricate references, flatten context, overstate language claims, or sound certain when evidence is mixed. Confidence is not proof.

How to verify an answer

  1. Read the entire paragraph or chapter, not only the selected verse.
  2. Open every cited biblical reference and confirm that it supports the statement.
  3. Compare translations when wording affects the conclusion.
  4. Check Greek or Hebrew claims with an interlinear, lexicon, grammar, or qualified commentary.
  5. Compare responsible interpretations when Christians disagree.
  6. Take consequential pastoral, medical, legal, safety, or mental-health questions to qualified humans.

Source hierarchy

We begin with the passage and its literary context. Depending on the question, responsible study may also require cross-references, translation notes, original-language reference works, historical and cultural sources, and clearly identified commentaries. A dictionary definition alone does not determine a word's meaning in context.

Bible translations and quotations

Translation availability and quotation permissions vary. Direct quotations should identify the translation where practical and comply with its publisher's rules. Paraphrases should not be formatted or described as direct Scripture quotations.

Corrections

Report a suspected error to support@mybiblecopilot.com. Include the URL or app screen, the disputed statement, why it may be wrong, and supporting sources. Substantive website corrections should update the visible review date where the page supports one.

Independent theological review

We are building a disclosed reviewer network. Participation should never be described as an endorsement of the entire product. Reviewers may assess a defined sample, methodology, policy, or subject area, and Bible Copilot should publish the boundaries of that work.

Relationships in the Bible: What Every Christian Should Know

By Bible Copilot Updated July 3, 2026 Scripture-cited · editorial standards
Quick answer

Discover what every Christian should know about relationships through Bible teaching. Explore biblical principles for healthy Christian relationships.

Introduction

Understanding relationships in the Bible provides Christian believers with a comprehensive framework for approaching the diverse connections that form our lives. Relationships in the Bible aren't presented as peripheral concerns—they stand central to God's design for human flourishing and spiritual development. What every Christian should know about relationships begins with recognizing that Scripture addresses them extensively, offering wisdom about family bonds, friendships, romantic partnerships, and community connections. This exploration of relationships in the Bible reveals what Christians should know to navigate human connection with wisdom grounded in God's Word.

Relationships in the Bible are presented not as optional or luxury aspects of Christian life but as essential to who we are and who we become. What every Christian should know about relationships includes understanding that God is fundamentally relational—He exists in community (the Trinity), creates us for community, and redeems us into community through the church. Learning what the Bible teaches about relationships equips believers to build connections that honor God, reflect Christ's love, and contribute to mutual spiritual growth.

The Foundation: God Designs Relationships

What every Christian should know about relationships in the Bible begins with recognizing that God designed them. Genesis 2:18 establishes this: "The Lord God said, 'It is not good for the man to be alone. I will make a helper suitable for him.'" Relationships in the Bible aren't presented as human invention but as divine design. From humanity's inception, God recognized that isolation is contrary to human flourishing.

This principle extends beyond marriage to all relationships. What every Christian should know about relationships includes understanding that God designed us as relational beings. We're created for connection, community, and interdependence. Ecclesiastes 4:9-10 affirms this about relationships in the Bible: "Two are better than one, because they have a good return for their labor: If either of them falls down, one can help the other up."

What every Christian should know about relationships recognizes that God is fundamentally relational. 1 John 4:7-8 teaches: "Dear friends, let us love one another, for love comes from God. Everyone who loves has been born of God and knows God... God is love." Relationships in the Bible reflect God's nature. When we relate to others with love, we're expressing something fundamental about the God who created us.

What Every Christian Should Know: The Purpose of Relationships

Relationships in the Bible serve multiple sacred purposes. First, they facilitate spiritual growth and transformation. Proverbs 27:17 teaches: "As iron sharpens iron, so one person sharpens another." What every Christian should know about relationships is that they refine character. Our connections with others help us develop virtue, overcome weakness, and become more like Christ.

Second, relationships in the Bible provide mutual support and encouragement. What every Christian should know about relationships includes recognizing our dependence on others. 1 Thessalonians 5:11 instructs: "Therefore encourage one another and build each other up, just as in fact you are doing." Relationships in the Bible are channels through which God provides encouragement, strength, and hope.

Third, relationships in the Bible demonstrate God's love to the world. John 13:34-35 records Jesus saying: "As I have loved you, so you must love one another. By this everyone will know that you are my disciples, if you love one another." What every Christian should know about relationships is that they're witnesses to God's character and love.

What Every Christian Should Know About Different Relationship Types

Relationships in the Bible include multiple distinct types, each with particular characteristics and purposes. Family relationships are foundational. Ephesians 5:25 addresses husbands: "Husbands, love your wives, just as Christ loved the church and gave himself up for her." What every Christian should know about family relationships includes understanding them as covenantal bonds requiring sacrificial love.

Friendship constitutes another essential relationship type. What every Christian should know about friendships includes that Scripture presents them as deeply significant. Proverbs 17:17 affirms: "A friend loves at all times, and a brother is born for adversity." Relationships in the Bible through friendship involve loyalty, mutual growth, and covenantal commitment.

Church relationships form another critical category. Hebrews 10:24-25 teaches: "And let us consider how we may spur one another on toward love and good deeds, not giving up meeting together, as some are in the habit of doing, but encouraging one another." What every Christian should know about church relationships is that regular gathering and mutual encouragement are essential to spiritual health.

What Every Christian Should Know: Honesty and Accountability in Relationships

Relationships in the Bible are strengthened through honest communication. What every Christian should know about relationships includes that truthfulness, even difficult truthfulness, reflects genuine care. Proverbs 27:5-6 teaches: "Better is open rebuke than hidden love. Wounds from a friend can be trusted, but an enemy multiplies kisses."

Proverbs 28:23 reinforces this understanding of relationships in the Bible: "Whoever rebukes a person will in the end gain favor than one who has a flattering tongue." What every Christian should know about relationships is that true friends care more about your growth than your approval. Honest feedback strengthens rather than weakens authentic relationships.

What every Christian should know about relationships also includes accountability. Galatians 6:2 teaches: "Carry each other's burdens, and in this way you will fulfill the law of Christ." Relationships in the Bible involve mutual accountability and support through life's struggles.

What Every Christian Should Know: Choosing Relationships Wisely

Relationships in the Bible require intentional selection. What every Christian should know about relationships includes the importance of wise companion selection. Proverbs 13:20 teaches: "Walk with the wise and become wise, for a companion of fools suffers harm." Relationships in the Bible shape us—we become like those with whom we spend time.

Proverbs 22:24-25 provides specific caution: "Do not make friends with an angry man, do not associate with one easily angered, or you may learn his ways and get yourself ensnared." What every Christian should know about relationships is that we should protect ourselves from those whose character patterns would harm us.

2 Corinthians 6:14 offers guidance about relationships in the Bible: "Do not be yoked together with unbelievers." What every Christian should know about relationships includes that your deepest bonds should be with those who share your faith and values.

What Every Christian Should Know: Forgiveness in Relationships

Relationships in the Bible inevitably involve conflict among imperfect people. What every Christian should know about relationships includes that forgiveness is essential to their survival. Colossians 3:13 teaches: "Bear with each other and forgive one another if any of you has a grievance against someone. Forgive as the Lord forgave you."

Matthew 18:15-17 provides guidance for resolving relational conflict. What every Christian should know about relationships is that direct, humble conversation can resolve most disagreements. Ephesians 4:2-3 reinforces this: "Be completely humble and gentle; be patient, bearing with one another in love. Make every effort to keep the unity of the Spirit through the bond of peace."

What Every Christian Should Know: God's Presence in Relationships

What every Christian should know about relationships includes that God is present within them. Matthew 18:20 promises: "For where two or three gather in my name, there am I with them." Relationships in the Bible aren't merely human connections—they're places where God's presence and blessing operate.

What every Christian should know about relationships also recognizes that God remains faithful when relationships disappoint. Psalm 55:22 offers comfort: "Cast your cares on him for he cares for you." When human relationships fail, God's faithful presence sustains us.

FAQ

Q: Does the Bible say all relationships must be with believers? A: You can be kind to everyone, but your deepest relationships should be with believers who share your faith. What every Christian should know is that intimate relationships affect your spiritual direction, making alignment on faith essential.

Q: How does the Bible help when relationships become difficult? A: Matthew 18:15-17 provides a process for addressing conflict. Colossians 3:13 teaches forgiveness. What every Christian should know is that relationships can survive difficulty through honest communication and grace.

Q: What should every Christian know about prioritizing relationships? A: God calls us to cultivate relationships intentionally. Hebrews 10:24-25 emphasizes gathering regularly. What every Christian should know is that investing in relationships reflects God's design for human flourishing.

Q: Can every Christian expect all relationships to succeed? A: No. Some relationships end or require distance. What every Christian should know is that grief over lost relationships is valid, and God remains present even when people disappoint us.

Q: How should every Christian approach unhealthy relationships? A: Proverbs 22:24-25 warns about toxic relationships. What every Christian should know is that protecting your spiritual wellbeing sometimes requires creating boundaries or distance from people whose influence harms you.

What God Says About Tithing: A Scripture-Based Guide

By Bible Copilot Updated July 3, 2026 Scripture-cited · editorial standards
Quick answer

Understand what God says about tithing through Scripture. This guide explores biblical teaching on tithes and their application in Christian life.

Introduction

What God says about tithing reveals much about His character and expectations for His people. Throughout Scripture, God presents tithing not primarily as financial obligation but as expression of faith, worship, and stewardship. What God says about tithing establishes clear practices in the Old Testament while allowing flexibility in New Testament application. This Scripture-based guide explores what God says about tithing across both testaments and how to understand and apply this teaching.

What God Says About Tithing: Foundation

What God says about tithing begins with recognition that He deserves our best. Leviticus 27:30 establishes: "A tithe of everything from the land, whether grain from the soil or fruit from the trees, belongs to the Lord; it is holy to the Lord." What God says about tithing makes clear that tithes are holy—set apart for divine purposes.

The Old Testament shows what God says about tithing supported multiple purposes. Numbers 18:21 explains that tithes went to Levites as inheritance "in return for the work they do while serving at the tent of meeting." What God says about tithing included ensuring that religious leaders could serve full-time.

What God says about tithing also addresses justice. Deuteronomy 14:28-29 describes how every third year, "the Levite, the foreigner, the fatherless and the widow who live in your towns shall come and eat and be satisfied." What God says about tithing shows it was designed to protect vulnerable populations.

What God Says About Tithing: Jesus's Perspective

What God says about tithing transforms in the New Testament. Jesus never commanded followers to tithe, yet He acknowledged the practice. Matthew 23:23 records Jesus saying: "You give a tenth of your spices—mint, dill and cumin. But you have neglected the more important matters of the law—justice, mercy and faithfulness."

What God says about tithing through Jesus is that tithe-giving alone, without justice and mercy, misses the point. Jesus elevated internal righteousness above external compliance.

Mark 12:41-44 illustrates what God says about tithing. Jesus observed givers contributing to the temple treasury. The widow who dropped two small coins—her entire livelihood—was praised more highly than wealthy donors. What God says about tithing is that sacrifice and sincerity matter more than amount.

Luke 12:33 expresses what God says about tithing: "Sell your possessions and give to the poor. Provide purses for yourselves that will not wear out, a treasure in heaven." What God says about tithing invites generosity potentially exceeding standard tithes.

What God Says About Tithing: Paul's Teaching

Paul developed what God says about tithing through a grace-motivated framework. Second Corinthians 9:7 states: "Each of you should give what you have decided in your heart to give, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver." What God says about tithing emphasizes attitude—joy and willingness matter supremely.

What God says about tithing in 2 Corinthians 8:1-7 celebrates generosity flowing from grace: "And now, just as you excel in everything—in faith, in speech, in knowledge, in complete earnestness and in your love for us—see that you also excel in this grace of giving."

First Corinthians 16:1-4 shows what God says about tithing practically: Paul recommended regular, proportional giving on the first day of the week. What God says about tithing through Paul emphasizes consistency and planning rather than percentage specifics.

What God Says About Tithing: Trust and Provision

What God says about tithing is grounded in trust in God's provision. Matthew 6:25-34 records Jesus teaching: "Do not worry about your life, what you will eat or drink... But seek first his kingdom and his righteousness, and all these things will be given to you as well."

What God says about tithing through Jesus assumes believers can give generously because God cares for their needs. He illustrates through nature: "Look at the birds of the air; they do not sow or reap... and yet your heavenly Father feeds them. Are you not much more valuable than they?"

Philippians 4:19 promises what God says about tithing: "And my God will meet all your needs according to the riches of his glory in Christ Jesus." What God says about tithing assures sufficiency for faithful givers.

Luke 6:38 captures what God says about tithing: "Give, and it will be given to you. A good measure, pressed down, shaken together and running over, will be poured into your lap." What God says about tithing is that giving produces return.

What God Says About Tithing: Promises

Malachi 3:8-12 presents what God says about tithing with remarkable promises. God expresses concern about those withholding tithes: "Will a man rob God?... In tithes and offerings. You are under a curse—the whole nation of you—because you are robbing me."

But those who tithe faithfully receive blessing: "Bring the whole tithe into the storehouse... and see if I will not throw open the floodgates of heaven and pour out so much blessing that there will not be room enough to store it." What God says about tithing includes both warning and promise.

Proverbs 3:9-10 expresses what God says about tithing: "Honor the Lord with your wealth, with the firstfruits of all your crops; then your barns will be filled to overflowing." What God says about tithing connects giving to blessing.

What God Says About Tithing: Justice and Compassion

What God says about tithing is inseparable from justice. Proverbs 31:8-9 calls: "Speak up for those who cannot speak for themselves, for the rights of all who are destitute." What God says about tithing includes advocating for the vulnerable.

First John 3:17 makes clear what God says about tithing: "If anyone has material possessions and sees a brother or sister in need but has no pity on them, how can the love of God be in that person?" What God says about tithing demands action toward those in need.

Matthew 25:31-46 describes the final judgment based on what God says about tithing. Jesus evaluates people on how they cared for the hungry, thirsty, stranger, naked, and sick. What God says about tithing makes this fundamental to authentic faith.

What God Says About Tithing: Worship

What God says about tithing presents it as worship. Hebrews 13:15-16 teaches: "Through Jesus, therefore, let us continually offer to God a sacrifice of praise... And do not forget to do good and to share with others, for with such sacrifices God is pleased."

Philippians 4:18 describes gifts as "a fragrant offering, an acceptable sacrifice, pleasing to God." What God says about tithing uses sacrificial language—tithes offered in faith rise as worship to God's throne.

FAQ

Q: What does God say about tithing—is it required for Christians today? A: What God says about tithing in the Old Testament was a command. The New Testament doesn't explicitly require tithes but emphasizes proportional, faithful giving. The principle remains important; application varies.

Q: What does God say about tithing—should it always be ten percent? A: What God says about tithing in the Old Testament specified ten percent. The New Testament emphasizes proportional giving without mandating specific percentages. Many find ten percent helpful; others give differently based on conviction and capacity.

Q: What does God says about tithing—if I disagree with my church's use of funds? A: What God says about tithing emphasizes personal conviction and wise discernment. You can support your church while directing additional gifts to causes aligned with biblical priorities for justice and the Gospel.

Q: What does God say about tithing—when I can't afford ten percent? A: What God says about tithing emphasizes proportional, joyful giving. Give what you can. As capacity increases, tithing can grow. God values the willing heart.

Q: What does God say about tithing—about eternal reward? A: What God says about tithing promises that giving creates heavenly treasure. Matthew 6:20 teaches to "store up for yourselves treasures in heaven." Your tithes produce eternal investment.

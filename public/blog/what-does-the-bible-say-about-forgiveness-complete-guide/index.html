What Does the Bible Say About Forgiveness? (Complete Guide)

By Bible Copilot Updated July 3, 2026 Scripture-cited · editorial standards
Quick answer

Get the complete guide to what the Bible says about forgiveness with verses, teachings, and practical wisdom for your spiritual journey.

The question "What does the Bible say about forgiveness?" deserves a comprehensive answer because Scripture's teaching on this topic is extensive, nuanced, and deeply relevant to Christian living. What does the Bible say about forgiveness touches on God's character, Jesus' teachings, Paul's practical guidance, and the lived experience of biblical figures. This complete guide explores what the Bible says about forgiveness across both testaments, answering common questions and providing the scriptural foundation you need to understand and practice forgiveness in your own life.

What Does the Bible Say About Forgiveness: The Foundation

What does the Bible say about forgiveness begins with God's character and His forgiveness toward us. Understanding what the Bible says about forgiveness requires starting here.

Isaiah 43:25 declares what God says about forgiveness: "I, even I, am he who blots out your transgressions, for my own sake, and remembers your sins no more." What the Bible says about forgiveness shows that God actively chooses not to remember sins. This isn't grudging forgiveness but complete removal of offense.

Psalm 103:10 celebrates what the Bible says about forgiveness: "He does not treat us as our sins deserve or repay us according to our iniquities." What the Bible says about forgiveness is that it's far more generous than justice would require.

Romans 5:8 explains what the Bible says about forgiveness: "But God demonstrates his own love for us in this: While we were still sinners, Christ died for us." What the Bible says about forgiveness shows that God forgave us while we were actively rebelling—before we repented, changed, or deserved it.

This foundation is essential because what the Bible says about forgiveness toward others flows from understanding God's forgiveness toward us.

What Does the Bible Say About Forgiveness: Jesus' Teaching

What Jesus taught about forgiveness is central to understanding what the Bible says about forgiveness.

Matthew 6:12, 14-15 shows what the Bible says about forgiveness through prayer: "Forgive us our debts, as we also have forgiven our debtors... For if you forgive other people when they sin against you, your heavenly Father will also forgive you. But if you do not forgive others their sins, your Father will not forgive your sins." What the Bible says about forgiveness connects it directly to our spiritual standing.

Matthew 18:21-35 teaches what the Bible says about forgiveness through a parable. When asked how many times to forgive, Jesus responded with "seventy-seven times" and then told of a servant forgiven a huge debt who refused to forgive a small debt. What the Bible says about forgiveness shows that comparison should motivate our willingness to forgive.

Luke 6:27-28, 37 reveals what the Bible says about forgiveness: "Love your enemies, do good to those who hate you, bless those who curse you... Do not judge, and you will not be judged. Do not condemn, and you will not be condemned. Forgive, and you will be forgiven." What the Bible says about forgiveness extends even to enemies.

Luke 23:34 shows what the Bible says about forgiveness in practice. As Jesus was crucified, He prayed: "Father, forgive them, for they do not know what they are doing." What the Bible says about forgiveness reaches its ultimate expression in Christ's prayer for His killers.

John 8:1-11 demonstrates what the Bible says about forgiveness when a woman caught in adultery faced execution. Jesus said: "Neither do I condemn you... Go now and leave your life of sin." What the Bible says about forgiveness includes both grace and the call to transformation.

What Does the Bible Say About Forgiveness: Paul's Practical Teaching

Paul's letters develop what the Bible says about forgiveness for Christian communities.

Ephesians 4:31-32 summarizes what the Bible says about forgiveness: "Get rid of all bitterness, rage and anger, brawling and slander, and every form of malice. Be kind and compassionate to one another, forgiving each other, just as in Christ God forgave you." What the Bible says about forgiveness includes practical steps and the model of Christ's forgiveness.

Colossians 3:12-15 extends what the Bible says about forgiveness: "Therefore, as God's chosen people, holy and dearly loved, clothe yourselves with compassion, kindness, humility, gentleness and patience. Bear with each other and forgive whatever grievances you may have against one another. Forgive as the Lord forgave you. And over all these virtues put on love, which binds them all together in perfect unity. Let the peace of Christ rule in your hearts." What the Bible says about forgiveness places it within Christian character development and promises peace.

Romans 12:19-21 explains what the Bible says about forgiveness and justice: "Do not take revenge, my dear friends, but leave room for God's wrath... On the contrary: 'If your enemy is hungry, feed him; if he is thirsty, give him something to drink... Do not be overcome by evil, but overcome evil with good.'" What the Bible says about forgiveness rejects personal vengeance while embracing active goodness.

2 Corinthians 5:18-20 reveals what the Bible says about forgiveness as reconciliation: "All this is from God, who reconciled us to himself through Christ and gave us the ministry of reconciliation... We are therefore Christ's ambassadors, as though God were making his appeal through us." What the Bible says about forgiveness makes it central to Christian mission.

What Does the Bible Say About Forgiveness: The Warnings

What the Bible says about forgiveness includes warnings about what happens when we fail to forgive.

Hebrews 12:14-15 warns what happens to communities when people don't forgive: "Make every effort to live in peace with everyone and to be holy... See to it that no one falls short of the grace of God and that no bitter root grows up to cause trouble and defile many." What the Bible says about forgiveness shows that unforgiveness spreads damage.

Mark 11:25-26 connects what the Bible says about forgiveness to prayer: "And when you stand praying, if you hold anything against anyone, forgive them, so that your Father in heaven may forgive your sins." What the Bible says about forgiveness indicates that grudges block answered prayer.

Proverbs 17:9 teaches what the Bible says about forgiveness and relationships: "Whoever would foster love covers over an offense, but whoever repeats the matter separates close friends." What the Bible says about forgiveness shows that rehearsing wrongs prevents healing.

What Does the Bible Say About Forgiveness: Clarifications

What the Bible says about forgiveness often contradicts popular misconceptions.

What the Bible says about forgiveness is that it doesn't require forgetting. Remembering isn't unforgiveness; holding grudges is. What the Bible says about forgiveness means choosing not to hold offense against someone, even when you remember what happened.

What the Bible says about forgiveness shows it's distinct from reconciliation. You can forgive someone while maintaining boundaries or distance. What the Bible says about forgiveness is possible even in ended relationships.

What the Bible says about forgiveness doesn't mean the offense was okay or didn't matter. What the Bible says about forgiveness acknowledges real harm while releasing bitterness.

Proverbs 27:12 shows that what the Bible says about forgiveness includes wisdom: "The prudent see danger and take refuge, but the simple keep going and pay the penalty." What the Bible says about forgiveness supports taking protective steps.

What Does the Bible Say About Forgiveness: The Results

What the Bible says about forgiveness includes the benefits of choosing to forgive.

Colossians 3:15 promises what happens when you forgive: "Let the peace of Christ rule in your hearts, since as members of one body you were called to peace." What the Bible says about forgiveness promises internal peace.

John 8:36 declares what the Bible says about forgiveness's liberating power: "So if the Son sets you free, you are free indeed." What the Bible says about forgiveness offers genuine freedom.

Psalm 32:1-2 celebrates what the Bible says about forgiveness's blessing: "Blessed is the one whose transgressions are forgiven, whose sins are covered. Blessed is the one whose sin the Lord does not count against them and in whose spirit is no deceit." What the Bible says about forgiveness shows it brings deep happiness.

FAQ

Q: What does the Bible say about forgiveness when someone never repents? A: The Bible says you forgive regardless of the other person's repentance. Your forgiveness depends on your obedience, not their response.

Q: What does the Bible say about forgiving the same person repeatedly? A: The Bible says forgiveness should be unlimited (Matthew 18:22). However, what the Bible says about forgiveness is compatible with wise boundaries.

Q: What does the Bible say about whether forgiveness is required? A: The Bible clearly commands forgiveness. What the Bible says about forgiveness makes it a non-negotiable Christian practice.

Q: What does the Bible say about self-forgiveness? A: The Bible doesn't explicitly address self-forgiveness, but what the Bible says about God's complete forgiveness provides the model.

Q: What does the Bible say about how long forgiveness takes? A: The Bible says the choice to forgive can be made quickly, but what the Bible says about forgiveness suggests emotional healing often takes time.

Top 25 Bible Verses About Children (With Explanations)

By Bible Copilot Updated July 3, 2026 Scripture-cited · editorial standards
Quick answer

Explore the top 25 Bible verses about children with explanations. Find biblical wisdom on child-rearing, value, and spiritual growth.

Introduction

Scripture contains numerous verses about children that address their value, needs, potential, and the responsibilities of adults toward them. These Bible verses about children offer comfort to anxious parents, direction to those seeking guidance, and perspective to those questioning children's role in God's kingdom. Whether you're a parent, educator, or simply someone interested in Scripture's view of childhood, these Bible verses about children provide foundational insight. The Bible verses about children we've selected represent the most essential passages addressing childhood across Scripture. Studying these Bible verses about children helps Christian families understand their role in raising the next generation and God's vision for children.

25 Essential Bible Verses About Children

1. Psalm 127:3

"Children are a heritage from the Lord, offspring a reward from him."

This foundational verse establishes that children are divine gifts—inheritances and rewards rather than burdens or interruptions. This perspective transforms how we receive and treat children.

2. Matthew 19:14

"Jesus said, 'Let the little children come to me, and do not hinder them, for the kingdom of heaven belongs to such as these.'"

Jesus's welcoming of children reveals their present value in His kingdom. This verse demonstrates that children aren't waiting for future importance—they matter now.

3. Proverbs 22:6

"Start children off on the way they should go; and even when they are old, they will not turn from it."

This verse emphasizes that early parental guidance creates lasting patterns. Childhood instruction echoes throughout a lifetime.

4. Deuteronomy 6:4-7

"Love the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your strength. These commandments that I give you today are to be on your hearts. Impress them on your children. Talk about them when you sit at home and when you walk along the road, when you lie down and when you get up."

This passage establishes that spiritual formation happens through everyday conversation and example, woven into daily family life.

5. Ephesians 6:4

"Fathers, do not exasperate your children; instead, bring them up in the training and instruction of the Lord."

Paul calls for patient, intentional spiritual guidance rather than harsh, frustrating parenting that damages children.

6. Colossians 3:21

"Fathers, do not embitter your children, or they will become discouraged."

This verse warns against treatment that crushes children's spirits, emphasizing that how we treat children profoundly affects them.

7. Proverbs 13:24

"Whoever spares the rod hates their children, but the one who loves their children is careful to discipline them."

This verse connects loving children with maintaining boundaries and teaching consequences through discipline.

8. Proverbs 29:15

"The rod and reproof give wisdom, but a child left to himself brings shame to his mother."

This verse emphasizes that guidance and correction, paired with freedom, help children develop wisdom.

9. Proverbs 22:15

"Folly is bound up in the heart of a child, but the rod of discipline drives it far away."

This verse acknowledges that poor judgment is natural to childhood and that parental guidance helps children develop wisdom.

10. Proverbs 19:18

"Discipline your children, for in that there is hope; do not be a willing party to their death."

Discipline provides hope by teaching children to make wise choices. Permissiveness leaves them unprepared for life.

11. Matthew 18:6

"If anyone causes one of these little ones...to stumble, it would be better for them to have a large millstone hung around their neck and to be drowned in the depths of the sea."

Jesus's extreme language reveals how seriously God takes the protection and wellbeing of children.

12. 1 Timothy 4:12

"Don't let anyone look down on you because you are young, but set an example for the believers in all you do, in speech, in conduct, in love, in faith and in purity."

This verse addresses young believers, suggesting they can exemplify faith even in youth.

13. Proverbs 31:28

"Her children arise and call her blessed; her husband also, and he praises her."

This verse describes the legacy of a wise mother whose children naturally express gratitude and honor.

14. 1 John 3:18

"Dear children, let us not love with words or speech but with actions and in truth."

Love toward children must be demonstrated concretely through actions, not just expressed verbally.

15. Proverbs 20:11

"Even small children are known by their actions, so is their conduct really all good?"

Children's character is evident in their behavior, which reflects the values they're being taught.

16. Proverbs 17:6

"Children's children are a crown to the aged, and parents are the pride of their children."

This verse describes the intergenerational blessing that flows from faithful parenting.

17. 2 Timothy 1:5

"I am reminded of your sincere faith, which first lived in your grandmother Lois and in your mother Eunice and, I am persuaded, now lives in you also."

Faith passes generationally, from grandparents to parents to children. Faithful parenting creates enduring legacy.

18. Psalm 139:13-14

"For you created my inmost being; you knit me together in my mother's womb. I praise you because I am fearfully and wonderfully made; your works are wonderful, I know that full well."

This verse affirms that God intentionally creates each child and values them as wonderful creations.

19. Matthew 18:3

"And he said: 'Truly I tell you, unless you change and become like little children, you will never enter the kingdom of heaven.'"

Jesus identifies childlike qualities—dependence, trust, openness—as essential to kingdom participation.

20. Proverbs 23:13-14

"Do not withhold discipline from a child; if you strike him with a rod, he will not die. If you strike him with the rod, you will save his soul from death."

In context, this verse emphasizes that appropriate correction can prevent destruction by teaching consequences early.

21. Proverbs 27:12

"The prudent see danger and take refuge, but the simple keep going and pay the penalty."

Parents teach prudence—the ability to anticipate consequences and adjust behavior accordingly.

22. Joshua 24:15

"But if serving the Lord seems undesirable to you, then choose for yourselves this day whom you will serve...But as for me and my household, we will serve the Lord."

Parents declare the spiritual direction of their households, establishing faith as central.

23. Proverbs 14:12

"There is a way that appears to be right, but in the end it leads to death."

Parents guide children away from paths that seem good but lead to destruction.

24. Hebrews 12:7-10

"Endure hardship as discipline; God is treating you as his children...How much more should we submit to the Father of spirits and live!"

Discipline is evidence of belonging and relationship. God disciplines us because we matter to Him.

25. Mark 9:36-37

"He took a little child whom he placed among them. Taking the child in his arms, he said to them, 'Whoever welcomes one of these little children in my name welcomes me.'"

Jesus equated welcoming and honoring a child with welcoming and honoring Him.

FAQ

Q: Are these verses culturally outdated? A: The cultural expressions reflect ancient times, but the principles about children's value, parental responsibility, and guidance remain eternally relevant.

Q: Should I memorize all 25 verses? A: Start with a few that speak to your current parenting needs. Meditating on a handful of verses deeply is more effective than superficially knowing many.

Q: How do I handle verses about discipline that trouble me? A: Consider cultural context and consult commentaries. The principle—that children benefit from guidance and consequences—applies across cultures. Specific implementation methods can vary.

Q: Can single parents apply these verses effectively? A: Yes. These verses address parental principles applicable regardless of family structure. God empowers parents in all circumstances.

Q: Which verses address specific age groups? A: While not age-specific, verses about discipline apply to all ages; verses about spiritual formation address childhood particularly; verses about consequences address older children able to understand cause and effect.

What God Says About Marriage: A Scripture-Based Guide

By Bible Copilot Updated July 3, 2026 Scripture-cited · editorial standards
Quick answer

Learn what God says about marriage directly from Scripture. A comprehensive guide to biblical marriage principles and God's design for couples.

Introduction

What God says about marriage provides the authoritative foundation for understanding this sacred relationship. Rather than turning to cultural trends, personal experience, or secular psychology, Christians should ground their marriages in God's revealed Word. What God says about marriage spans from creation to the New Testament church, offering consistent guidance about design, purpose, and practice. Understanding what God says about marriage transforms how you approach your relationship—from seeking personal happiness to pursuing mutual sanctification and glorifying God.

What God says about marriage is remarkable in its comprehensiveness and relevance. God addresses everything from purpose to roles to intimacy to conflict. What God says about marriage treats this relationship as central to human flourishing and spiritual development. By studying Scripture carefully, you discover that what God says about marriage is simultaneously challenging and comforting—calling couples to high standards while providing grace and hope for the journey.

What God Says About Marriage's Creation and Purpose

What God says about marriage begins in Genesis with purpose and design. Genesis 1:27-28 reveals God's intentional design: "So God created mankind in his own image, in the image of God he created them; male and female he created them. God blessed them and said to them, 'Be fruitful and increase in number; fill the earth and subdue it.'"

What God says about marriage shows that He created both genders as equal image-bearers. Marriage isn't an afterthought but central to God's creative design. The command to be fruitful indicates that children are often part of God's design, though not required for all unions. What God says about marriage establishes both as partners with shared calling and dignity.

Genesis 2:18-24 provides additional detail about what God says about marriage: "The Lord God said, 'It is not good for the man to be alone. I will make a helper suitable for him'... The man said, 'This is now bone of my bones and flesh of my flesh; she shall be called woman, for she was taken out of man.' That is why a man leaves his father and mother and is united to his wife, and they become one flesh."

What God says about marriage emphasizes companionship as primary purpose. Loneliness was the only thing God declared "not good" in the creation account. What God says about marriage indicates that humans need relationship—it's not failure or weakness. The concept of "helper" shows that marriage partners complete and strengthen one another. What God says about marriage shows that the "one flesh" union is permanent and all-encompassing.

What God Says About Marital Commitment and Covenant

What God says about marriage treats it as covenant, not contract. Malachi 2:14-16 reveals God's perspective on covenant-breaking: "You ask, 'Why?' It is because the Lord is acting as the witness between you and the wife of your youth, because you have broken faith with her, though she is your partner, the wife of your covenant... So be on your guard, and do not be unfaithful."

What God says about marriage shows that God Himself witnesses marital vows. This isn't merely human business but divine concern. Infidelity is covenant-breaking before God. What God says about marriage indicates that the permanence and seriousness of marriage reflect its covenantal nature.

Matthew 19:4-6 shows Jesus reaffirming what God says about marriage: "Haven't you read, that at the beginning the Creator made them male and female, and said, 'For this reason a man will leave his father and mother and be united to his wife, and the two will become one flesh'? So they are no longer two, but one flesh. Therefore what God has joined together, let no one separate."

What God says about marriage through Christ emphasizes permanence and divine participation. God actively joins couples together. What God says about marriage teaches that separation violates God's design. Though God permits divorce in limited circumstances (Matthew 19:9), what God says about marriage consistently presents faithfulness as the ideal.

What God Says About Husbands' Roles and Responsibilities

What God says about marriage addresses husbands' specific calling. Ephesians 5:25-28 articulates what God says about marriage for husbands: "Husbands, love your wives, just as Christ loved the church and gave himself up for her to make her holy, cleansing her by the washing with water through the word, and to present her to himself as a radiant church, without stain or wrinkle or any other blemish, but holy and blameless. In this same way, husbands ought to love their wives as their own bodies."

What God says about marriage demands that husbands love sacrificially, redemptively, and sacrificially. This is radical love—loving even when unappreciated, prioritizing the wife's spiritual growth, and building her up. What God says about marriage calls husbands to love more than personal comfort or convenience.

1 Peter 3:7 adds what God says about marriage regarding husbands: "Likewise, husbands, live with your wives in an understanding way, showing honor to the woman as the weaker vessel, since they are heirs with you of the grace of life, so that your prayers may not be hindered."

What God says about marriage teaches that husbands must deliberately learn their wives. Understanding requires attention, listening, and genuine interest in her perspective. What God says about marriage connects this understanding to prayer effectiveness—spiritual life depends on marital health. This creates powerful motivation.

Colossians 3:19 summarizes what God says about marriage for husbands: "Husbands, love your wives and do not be harsh with them." What God says about marriage prohibits controlling, demeaning, or violent behavior. Husbands' authority isn't to dominate but to lead with gentleness and care.

What God Says About Wives' Roles and Responsibilities

What God says about marriage addresses wives' distinct calling. Ephesians 5:22-24 articulates what God says about marriage for wives: "Wives, submit yourselves to your own husbands as you do to the Lord. For the husband is the head of the family, just as Christ is the head of the church, his body, of which he is the Savior. Now as the church submits to Christ, so also wives should submit to their husbands in everything."

What God says about marriage requires careful interpretation of submission. Biblical submission is voluntary alignment with a husband's spiritual leadership, not slavish obedience. It mirrors the church's willing submission to Christ—not forced but chosen. What God says about marriage shows that this submission occurs within a framework of mutual respect and love.

Ephesians 5:21 provides essential context for what God says about marriage: "Submit to one another out of reverence for Christ." What God says about marriage includes mutual submission—both partners yielding desires to serve one another. This prevents abuse and establishes partnership.

What God says about marriage through Proverbs 31 reveals wives' tremendous value. The portrayed virtuous woman is industrious, wise, generous, and strong. What God says about marriage celebrates that wives contribute economically, spiritually, and intellectually to their families. What God says about marriage affirms women's agency and importance.

1 Peter 3:3-6 adds what God says about marriage about wives: "Your beauty should not come from outward adornment... Rather, it should be that of your inner self, the unfading beauty of a gentle and quiet spirit, which is of great worth in God's sight."

What God says about marriage prioritizes inner character over external appearance. This doesn't forbid physical care but emphasizes that lasting beauty comes from spiritual development. What God says about marriage values the spiritual qualities that enable marriage to flourish.

What God Says About Sexual Intimacy in Marriage

What God says about marriage includes robust affirmation of sexual relation. Song of Songs 4:9-11 shows what God says about marriage through passionate language: "You have stolen my heart, my sister, my bride; you have stolen my heart with one glance of your eyes... How delightful is your love, my sister, my bride! How much more pleasing is your love than wine, and the fragrance of your perfume than any spice!"

What God says about marriage celebrates physical attraction and desire without shame. These biblical verses affirm sensual pleasure as part of marriage's goodness. What God says about marriage through Song of Songs shows that God created humans as embodied beings designed for sexual joy within marriage.

1 Corinthians 7:3-5 explains what God says about marriage regarding sexual duty: "The husband should fulfill his marital duty to his wife, and likewise the wife to her husband. The wife does not have authority over her own body but yields it to her husband. In the same way, the husband does not have authority over her own body but yields it to his wife. Do not deprive each other except perhaps by mutual consent and for a time, so that you may devote yourselves to prayer."

What God says about marriage treats sexual relation as non-negotiable and spiritually significant. Withholding sex violates the marriage covenant. What God says about marriage shows that regular sexual intimacy strengthens emotional bonds and prevents temptation. Couples should make intimacy a priority.

Hebrews 13:4 protects this area: "Marriage should be honored by all, and the marriage bed kept pure, for God will judge the adulterer and all the sexually immoral." What God says about marriage emphasizes sexual faithfulness as sacred. Infidelity devastates marriages; exclusive sexual relationship honors the covenant.

What God Says About Marriage Challenges and Resolution

What God says about marriage acknowledges difficulties. Ephesians 4:2-3 provides what God says about marriage for conflict: "Be completely humble and gentle; be patient, bearing with one another in love. Make every effort to keep the unity of the Spirit through the bond of peace."

What God says about marriage teaches that couples should pursue peace actively, not passively hope conflict disappears. Humility, gentleness, and patience are required virtues. What God says about marriage indicates that unity requires intentional effort.

Matthew 18:21-22 shows what God says about marriage about forgiveness: "Then Peter came to Jesus and asked, 'Lord, how many times shall I forgive my brother or sister who sins against me? Up to seven times?' Jesus answered, 'I tell you, not seven times, but seventy-seven times.'"

What God says about marriage makes unlimited forgiveness essential. What God says about marriage teaches that grudges poison relationships. Couples must forgive repeatedly to preserve intimacy and move forward. What God says about marriage frees couples from the burden of unforgiveness.

What God Says About Choosing a Spouse

What God says about marriage emphasizes the importance of spouse selection. 2 Corinthians 6:14 articulates what God says about marriage regarding interfaith unions: "Do not be unequally yoked with unbelievers. For what do they have in common? Or what fellowship can light have with darkness?"

What God says about marriage makes shared faith non-negotiable. The most important criterion for spouse selection is whether your partner shares your deepest commitments—your relationship with God. What God says about marriage suggests that spiritual incompatibility creates fundamental conflict that marriage cannot overcome.

Amos 3:3 emphasizes what God says about marriage about compatibility: "Do two walk together unless they have agreed to do so?" What God says about marriage requires agreement on core values and life direction. Couples walking in opposite spiritual directions cannot genuinely unite.

FAQ

Q: What God says about marriage—does He permit divorce? A: Matthew 19:9 shows Jesus permits divorce for sexual immorality. First Corinthians 7:15 permits it when an unbelieving spouse abandons. However, what God says about marriage emphasizes reconciliation and forgiveness as preferred to divorce.

Q: What does God say about marriage if spouses have differing denominations? A: What God says about marriage requires shared Christian faith. Different Christian denominations can work if both partners share commitment to Christ. However, what God says about marriage warns against major theological differences that create ongoing conflict.

Q: What does God say about marriage regarding remarriage after divorce? A: What God says about marriage permits remarriage in specific cases (Matthew 19:9, 1 Corinthians 7:15). Christians should study Scripture carefully and seek pastoral wisdom before remarrying, as what God says about marriage emphasizes the seriousness of marital vows.

Q: What God says about marriage—can single people be fulfilled? A: Yes. First Corinthians 7 affirms that singleness is a valid calling. What God says about marriage celebrates the relationship but doesn't mandate it. Fulfillment comes through relationship with Christ, whether single or married.

Q: What does God say about marriage if partners disagree on children? A: What God says about marriage doesn't mandate children. Proverbs celebrates children but also acknowledges that some marriages won't have them. Couples should discuss this before marrying and prayerfully work through disagreements.

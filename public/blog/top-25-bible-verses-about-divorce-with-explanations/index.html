Top 25 Bible Verses About Divorce (With Explanations)

By Bible Copilot Updated July 3, 2026 Scripture-cited · editorial standards
Quick answer

25 key Bible verses about divorce with full explanations, interpretations, and practical application for Christians facing this reality.

The Bible addresses divorce through multiple passages spanning both Old and New Testaments. Understanding the top Bible verses about divorce provides clarity on Scripture's teaching, context, and application. This comprehensive guide presents 25 key Bible verses about divorce with explanations, showing how they work together to reveal God's perspective on marriage breakdown, grounds for divorce, and His grace for the broken.

Key Bible Verses About Divorce: Foundational Teachings

1. Genesis 2:24 (NIV) "That is why a man leaves his father and mother and is united to his wife, and they become one flesh."

Explanation: This foundational Bible verse about divorce establishes God's design for marriage. The phrase "one flesh" describes a permanent, exclusive union. This verse is quoted throughout Scripture, emphasizing marriage's sacred permanence and making divorce's significance clear.

2. Malachi 2:16 (NIV) "'I hate divorce,' says the Lord God of Israel."

Explanation: This Bible verse about divorce reveals God's emotional response to broken marriages. God doesn't hate divorced people; He hates what damages His beloved people. This verse shows God's passion for protecting people from relationship destruction.

3. Matthew 19:4-6 (NIV) "Haven't you read that at the beginning the Creator made them male and female, and said, 'For this reason a man will leave his father and mother and be united to his wife, and the two will become one flesh'? So they are no longer two, but one flesh. Therefore what God has joined together, let no one separate."

Explanation: This Bible verse about divorce presents Jesus's affirmation of God's design. Jesus returned to Genesis's foundational principle, emphasizing that God joins couples together in marriage and intends permanence.

Bible Verses About Divorce: Jesus's Teaching on Grounds

4. Matthew 19:9 (NIV) "I tell you that anyone who divorces his wife, except for sexual immorality, and marries another woman commits adultery."

Explanation: This is the central Bible verse about divorce from Jesus. It affirms marriage permanence while providing one biblical exception: sexual immorality (porneia in Greek), encompassing adultery and serious sexual betrayal.

5. Mark 10:11-12 (NIV) "He answered, 'Anyone who divorces his wife and marries another woman commits adultery against her. And if she divorces her husband and marries another man, she commits adultery.'"

Explanation: This Bible verse about divorce presents Jesus's teaching about accountability. Mark's version emphasizes that both men and women are held to the same standard, revolutionary in first-century culture.

6. Luke 16:18 (NIV) "Anyone who divorces his wife and marries another woman commits adultery, and the man who marries a divorced woman commits adultery."

Explanation: This Bible verse about divorce reinforces the seriousness of the marriage covenant. Luke's version emphasizes that remarriage after unauthorized divorce constitutes adultery, taking marriage vows seriously.

Bible Verses About Divorce: Jesus's Teaching on Heart Condition

7. Matthew 19:8 (NIV) "Jesus replied, 'Moses permitted you to divorce your wives because your hearts were hard. But it was not this way from the beginning.'"

Explanation: This Bible verse about divorce reveals the root cause: human hardness of heart. Jesus identified that divorce results from unwillingness to love sacrificially, forgive, or work through difficulty.

8. Matthew 19:11-12 (NIV) "Jesus replied, 'Not everyone can accept this word, but only those to whom it has been given. For there are eunuchs who were born that way, and others who have been made that way by others—and others have renounced marriage because of the kingdom of heaven. The one who can accept this should accept it.'"

Explanation: This Bible verse about divorce acknowledges Jesus's teaching is demanding. He recognized that accepting His standard of marriage permanence requires spiritual maturity and divine enablement.

Bible Verses About Divorce: Paul's Teaching

9. 1 Corinthians 7:10-11 (NIV) "To the married I give this command (not I, but the Lord): A wife must not separate from her husband. But if she does, she must remain unmarried or else be reconciled to her husband. And a husband must not divorce his wife."

Explanation: This Bible verse about divorce reiterates Jesus's teaching while allowing for separation. Paul indicates that if separation occurs, reconciliation should be pursued rather than divorce and remarriage.

10. 1 Corinthians 7:12-15 (NIV) "To the rest I say this (I, not the Lord): If any brother has a wife who is not a believer, and she is willing to live with him, he must not divorce her. And if a woman has a husband who is not a believer, and he is willing to live with her, she must not divorce him... But if the unbeliever leaves, let him do so. A believing man or woman is not bound in such circumstances; God has called us to live in peace."

Explanation: This Bible verse about divorce identifies abandonment by an unbeliever as grounds for separation. The phrase "not bound" indicates the believing spouse is released from the covenant obligation when the unbeliever departs.

11. 1 Corinthians 7:27-28 (NIV) "Are you bound to a wife? Do not seek to be free. Are you free from a wife? Do not look for a wife. But if you do marry, you have not sinned; and if a virgin marries, she has not sinned."

Explanation: This Bible verse about divorce suggests remarriage is permissible in certain circumstances. Paul's language indicates that being "free" (presumably through legitimate divorce) allows remarriage without sin.

12. 1 Corinthians 7:39 (NIV) "A woman is bound to her husband as long as he lives. But if her husband dies, she is free to marry anyone she wishes, but he must belong to the Lord."

Explanation: This Bible verse about divorce establishes the principle that death dissolves the marriage bond. The parallel implies other serious circumstances might similarly dissolve the covenant.

Bible Verses About Divorce: Old Testament Foundation

13. Deuteronomy 24:1-4 (NIV) "If a man marries a woman who becomes displeasing to him because he finds something indecent about her, and he writes her a certificate of divorce, gives it to her and sends her from his house... then her first husband, who divorced her, is not allowed to marry her again after she has been defiled."

Explanation: This Bible verse about divorce shows Old Testament regulation of the practice. Rather than endorsing divorce, the law provided legal protections for women and boundaries around the practice.

14. Proverbs 5:15-20 (NIV) "Drink water from your own cistern, running water from your own well... May your fountain be blessed, and may you rejoice in the wife of your youth... Why, my son, be captivated by an adulteress? Why embrace the bosom of another man's wife?"

Explanation: This Bible verse about divorce (indirectly) warns against breaking covenant through infidelity. Proverbs emphasizes marital fidelity and satisfaction within marriage rather than seeking others.

15. Malachi 2:14-15 (NIV) "You ask, 'Why?' It is because the Lord is the witness between you and the wife of your youth. You have been unfaithful to her, though she is your partner, the wife of your covenant... Has not the Lord, made them one? In flesh and spirit they are his."

Explanation: This Bible verse about divorce emphasizes the covenant nature of marriage and God's witness to it. God takes the marriage covenant seriously and responds emotionally to its breaking.

Bible Verses About Divorce: Related Passages on Marriage and Fidelity

16. Hebrews 13:4 (NIV) "Marriage should be honored by all, and the marriage bed kept pure, for God will judge the adulterer and all the sexually immoral."

Explanation: This Bible verse about divorce (related to grounds) establishes that sexual purity in marriage is important. Adultery and sexual immorality are serious matters with spiritual consequences.

17. Ephesians 5:25-27 (NIV) "Husbands, love your wives, just as Christ loved the church and gave himself up for her to make her holy, cleansing her by the washing with water through the word, and to present her to himself as a radiant church, without stain or wrinkle or any other blemish, but holy and blameless."

Explanation: This Bible verse about divorce (showing marriage's purpose) reveals that marriage reflects Christ's relationship with the church. Husbands are called to sacrificial love similar to Christ's.

18. 1 Peter 3:7 (NIV) "Husbands, in the same way be considerate as you live with your wives, and treat them with respect as the weaker partner and as heirs with you of the gracious gift of life, so that nothing will hinder your prayers."

Explanation: This Bible verse about divorce (indirectly) teaches that marriage requires intentional care and respect. How husbands treat wives affects their spiritual relationship with God.

19. 1 Corinthians 11:3 (NIV) "Now I want you to realize that the head of every man is Christ, and the head of the woman is man, and the head of Christ is God."

Explanation: This Bible verse about divorce (establishing marriage structure) shows authority structure intended to protect and serve. Authority exists for protection and direction, not domination or abuse.

Bible Verses About Divorce: God's Grace and Healing

20. Romans 8:1 (NIV) "Therefore, there is now no condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus."

Explanation: This Bible verse about divorce offers hope. Divorced believers aren't condemned or disqualified from God's love. In Christ, you're fully accepted regardless of marital history.

21. Psalm 34:18 (NIV) "The Lord is close to the brokenhearted and saves those who are crushed in spirit."

Explanation: This Bible verse about divorce promises God's presence during devastation. God draws near to those experiencing divorce's pain; He doesn't withdraw or judge.

22. Psalm 103:10 (NIV) "He does not treat us as our sins deserve or repay us according to our iniquities."

Explanation: This Bible verse about divorce (in relation to grace) assures that God's mercy isn't proportionate to failures. His grace exceeds our sin.

23. 2 Corinthians 5:17 (NIV) "Therefore, if anyone is in Christ, the new creation has come: The old has gone, the new is here!"

Explanation: This Bible verse about divorce affirms redemption. Your past, including divorce, doesn't define your future in God's eyes. In Christ, you're a new creation.

24. Isaiah 40:31 (NIV) "But those who hope in the Lord will renew their strength. They will soar on wings like eagles; they will run and not grow weary, they will walk and not be faint."

Explanation: This Bible verse about divorce (related to healing) promises renewed strength through trusting God. Healing from divorce is possible through faith in God's restoration.

25. Matthew 22:37-40 (NIV) "Jesus replied: 'Love the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your mind.' This is the first and greatest commandment. And the second is like it: 'Love your neighbor as yourself.' All the Law and the Prophets hang on these two commandments."

Explanation: This Bible verse about divorce (establishing ultimate principle) shows that all biblical teaching, including on divorce, derives from love for God and others. Decisions about divorce should be rooted in love and protection of people.

FAQ

Q: Which Bible verses about divorce did Jesus teach? A: Jesus taught on divorce primarily in Matthew 19:3-9, Mark 10:2-12, and Luke 16:18. These passages establish the foundation for Christian understanding of divorce.

Q: What Bible verses about divorce establish grounds? A: Matthew 19:9 mentions sexual immorality as grounds. 1 Corinthians 7:15 identifies abandonment by an unbeliever. Other Bible verses don't explicitly name additional grounds, though principles suggest abuse qualifies.

Q: Do Bible verses about divorce condemn divorced people? A: No. While Bible verses about divorce take marriage seriously, verses like Romans 8:1 and Psalm 34:18 show God's grace extends to divorced individuals.

Q: How many Bible verses about divorce are there? A: Dozens of Bible verses address divorce directly or relate to it. The 25 included here represent the most significant teaching on the topic.

Q: Should Bible verses about divorce determine my decision? A: Bible verses about divorce should inform your understanding, but you should also seek pastoral counsel, marriage counseling, and prayer. Application requires wisdom in your specific situation.

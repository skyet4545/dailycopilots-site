What God Says About Dating: A Scripture-Based Guide

By Bible Copilot Updated July 3, 2026 Scripture-cited · editorial standards
Quick answer

What God says about dating is found throughout Scripture. Discover biblical principles for healthy relationships and God-honoring romantic choices.

What God Says About Dating: The Foundation

What God says about dating is often misunderstood. Many believe Scripture condemns dating or offers little guidance for modern relationships. However, what God says about dating is deeply relevant and remarkably wise. While Scripture doesn't provide a modern dating manual, what God says about dating comes through clear principles about relationships, commitment, purity, and character.

Understanding what God says about dating helps you approach romantic relationships with confidence and faith. What God says about dating emphasizes that He cares about your relationships because He cares about your wholeness, your happiness, and your spiritual development. These three matter profoundly to Him.

What God Says About Dating's Foundation: Guard Your Heart

Proverbs 4:23

"Above all else, guard your heart, for everything you do flows from it."

What God says about dating begins with protecting your emotional and spiritual core. Your heart—your desires, vulnerabilities, wounds, hopes—is precious. What God says about dating is that your heart matters enough to protect proactively.

Guarding your heart in dating means: - Being thoughtful about who you invite into your emotional space - Not giving complete emotional access before someone has proven trustworthy - Recognizing when you're overlooking red flags due to attachment - Maintaining your own identity and values within the relationship

What God says about dating through this verse is that self-protection isn't selfish—it's wisdom. Your heart influences everything you do, so protecting it protects your future.

What God Says About Dating's Most Critical Factor: Faith Alignment

2 Corinthians 6:14-15

"Do not be unequally yoked with unbelievers. For what partnership has righteousness with lawlessness? Or what fellowship has light with darkness?"

What God says about dating's most critical compatibility factor is faith. This verse isn't obscure or outdated—it directly addresses dating decisions. What God says about dating non-Christians is that fundamental misalignment creates problems that transcend other compatibility.

Why does what God says about dating emphasize faith so strongly?

Because your faith shapes: - Your morality and decision-making framework - How you handle conflict and forgiveness - Your financial priorities and generosity - Your approach to family and parenting - How you treat others and yourself

What God says about dating reveals that someone can be kind, attractive, and fun while still being spiritually incompatible. The deepest compatibility requires shared spiritual foundation.

What God Says About Dating's Physical Dimension

1 Corinthians 6:18-20

"Flee from sexual immorality. All other sins a person commits are outside the body, but whoever sins sexually, sins against their own body. Do you not know that your bodies are temples of the Holy Spirit, who is in you, whom you have received from God? You are not your own; you were bought at a price. Therefore honor God with your bodies."

What God says about dating and physical boundaries is that your body matters. This isn't about shame—God created sexuality. What God says about dating is that sexual intimacy is sacred and belongs within marriage.

What God says about dating sexually includes: - Sexual intimacy outside marriage contradicts biblical values - Your body is the Holy Spirit's temple—its use has spiritual significance - Saving sexual intimacy for marriage elevates and protects it - Honoring God with your body is an act of worship

Hebrews 13:4

"Marriage should be honored by all, and the marriage bed kept pure, for God will judge the adulterer and all the sexually immoral."

What God says about dating's physical boundaries through this verse emphasizes that the marriage covenant is sacred. What God says about dating makes clear that sexual relationships belong within that covenant.

What God Says About Dating's Relationship to Your Faith

Matthew 6:33

"But seek first his kingdom and his righteousness, and all these things will be given to you as well."

What God says about dating through this verse is that your primary relationship should be with Him, not with a romantic partner. What God says about dating emphasizes prioritizing your faith above your dating life.

This doesn't mean avoiding relationships. Rather, what God says about dating is: - Don't pursue romance at the expense of your relationship with God - Don't compromise your values to maintain a dating relationship - Don't expect a person to fulfill what only God can fulfill - Trust that when you prioritize God, He provides what's genuinely good

What God Says About Dating's Need for Intentionality

Proverbs 22:3

"The prudent see danger and take refuge, but the simple keep going and pay the penalty."

What God says about dating is that wisdom requires intentionality. What God says about dating through this verse is that noticing red flags early and acting on them protects you.

What God says about dating includes being: - Clear about what you're looking for in a partner - Honest about your intentions with potential partners - Willing to end relationships that aren't aligned with your values - Thoughtful about how much you compromise to keep someone

What God Says About Dating: Love as Choice

1 Corinthians 13:4-8

"Love is patient, love is kind. It does not envy, it does not boast, it is not proud. It does not dishonor others, it is not self-seeking, it is not easily angered, it keeps no record of wrongs. Love does not delight in evil but rejoices with the truth. It always protects, always trusts, always hopes, always perseveres. Love never fails."

What God says about dating and love is that it's not primarily a feeling—it's a commitment and a choice. What God says about dating reminds us that initial attraction fades and feelings fluctuate, but real love persists.

What God says about dating through this passage is that you should evaluate potential partners by asking: Do they demonstrate patient, kind, non-boastful, humble, respectful, generous, slow-to-anger, forgiving love toward you and others?

What God Says About Dating: Seek Wise Counsel

Proverbs 15:22

"Plans fail for lack of counsel, but with many advisers they succeed."

What God says about dating is that relationships shouldn't be navigated alone. What God says about dating emphasizes the value of community wisdom through: - Trusted mentors - Friends who know you well - Pastoral leaders at your church - Older believers with successful relationships

What God says about dating makes clear that isolation in relationship decisions is unwise. Others can see what you're too emotionally invested to notice.

What God Says About Dating During Difficult Seasons

2 Timothy 2:22

"Flee the evil desires of youth and pursue righteousness, faith, love and peace, along with those who call on the Lord out of a pure heart."

What God says about dating when you're struggling with temptation is to actively flee. What God says about dating isn't passive—it requires intentional action to: - Create physical boundaries that prevent temptation - Limit alone time in private settings - Spend dating time in community - Pray for strength and wisdom

What God says about dating also emphasizes pursuing positive alternatives—righteousness, faith, love, peace—rather than just avoiding negative ones.

What God Says About Dating's Timing

Psalm 27:14

"Wait for the Lord; be strong and take heart and wait for the Lord."

What God says about dating when you're waiting for the right person is to trust God's timing. What God says about dating includes: - God's timing is better than your rushed timeline - Waiting well means investing in yourself, your faith, and your community - Desperation to find someone often leads to poor choices - Trust that if marriage is part of God's plan, He will provide

What God Says About Dating After Heartbreak

Psalm 147:3

"He heals the brokenhearted and binds up their wounds."

What God says about dating includes seasons of heartbreak. What God says about dating when relationships end is that healing is possible. What God says about dating after loss involves: - Grieving openly without shame - Seeking community support - Returning to your relationship with God - Learning from what happened - Trusting God's faithfulness

FAQ

Q: What God says about dating—is any of it outdated? A: What God says about dating addresses human hearts, which haven't fundamentally changed. The principles remain relevant across eras and cultures.

Q: What God says about dating—does it apply to online dating? A: Yes. What God says about dating applies whether you meet in person or online. Faith alignment, purity, intentionality, and character assessment remain essential.

Q: What God says about dating—can I date someone I hope will convert to Christianity? A: What God says about dating through 2 Corinthians 6:14 advises against it. While conversion is possible, beginning a relationship counting on someone changing spiritually is unwise.

Q: What God says about dating—how long should I date before marriage? A: What God says about dating provides principles, not timelines. Most relationship experts suggest 1-2 years allows sufficient time to assess compatibility through various seasons.

Q: What God says about dating—what if I've already made mistakes? A: What God says about dating includes grace. 1 John 1:9 promises forgiveness for those who confess their sins. Move forward with renewed commitment to biblical principles.

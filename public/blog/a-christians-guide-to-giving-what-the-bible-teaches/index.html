A Christian's Guide to Giving: What the Bible Teaches

By Bible Copilot Updated July 3, 2026 Scripture-cited · editorial standards
Quick answer

Discover a Christian's guide to giving based on Bible teaching. Learn scriptural principles, practical wisdom, and how to develop a theology of generosity grounded in Scripture.

Introduction

A Christian's guide to giving must be rooted in Scripture rather than cultural expectations or institutional pressure. What the Bible teaches about giving presents a vision of generosity motivated by love, grounded in trust, and expressing gratitude for God's provision. This guide for Christians explores what the Bible teaches about giving across both testaments, addressing fundamental questions: Why give? How much? To whom? With what attitude? From prayer to practical application, a Christian's guide to giving helps you develop a biblical theology of generosity that transforms your relationship with money and possessions.

Foundation: What the Bible Teaches About Giving

A Christian's guide to giving begins with understanding God's generous character. What the Bible teaches about giving reveals that God is fundamentally generous. Genesis 1 describes God giving creation abundantly—plants, animals, light, and land. John 3:16 captures what the Bible teaches about giving through God's ultimate sacrifice: "For God so loved the world that he gave his one and only Son."

A Christian's guide to giving teaches that we give because God gave first. Romans 12:1 presents what the Bible teaches about giving as response to grace: "Therefore, I urge you, brothers and sisters, in view of God's mercy, to offer your bodies as a living sacrifice, holy and pleasing to God—this is your true and proper worship."

What the Bible teaches about giving emphasizes that generosity reflects our deepest values. Second Corinthians 9:7 states what the Bible teaches about giving: "Each of you should give what you have decided in your heart to give, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver." A Christian's guide to giving prioritizes joy and willingness.

What the Bible Teaches About Giving: Motivation Matters

What the Bible teaches about giving repeatedly addresses heart motivation. The widow's offering in Mark 12:41-44 illustrates what the Bible teaches about giving powerfully. While wealthy donors gave ostentatiously from abundance, she gave two small coins—her entire livelihood. Jesus praised her, teaching that what the Bible teaches about giving values sacrifice and sincerity over amount.

A Christian's guide to giving must examine motivation. First Corinthians 13:3 warns what the Bible teaches about giving: "If I give all I possess to the poor and give over my body to hardship that I might boast, but do not have love, I gain nothing."

What the Bible teaches about giving in Matthew 6:1-4 cautions against giving for recognition: "Be careful not to do your 'acts of righteousness' before others... But when you give to the needy, do not let your left hand know what your right hand is doing, so that your giving may be in secret." A Christian's guide to giving emphasizes giving humbly without seeking credit.

What the Bible Teaches About Giving: Old Testament Structure

A Christian's guide to giving must understand Old Testament foundations. What the Bible teaches about giving in the Old Testament established clear structures:

The Tithe: Leviticus 27:30 states, "A tithe of everything from the land... belongs to the Lord; it is holy to the Lord." What the Bible teaches about giving through tithing required ten percent of agricultural income.

Firstfruits: Exodus 23:16 required offering "the firstfruits of the crops you plant." What the Bible teaches about giving through firstfruits emphasized offering the best portion first, honoring God's priority.

Freewill Offerings: Beyond mandatory giving, Leviticus 23:37 describes additional voluntary offerings. What the Bible teaches about giving included opportunities to exceed minimum requirements based on conviction.

A Christian's guide to giving recognizes that the Old Testament system supported religious leadership, provided for vulnerable populations, and funded communal worship. What the Bible teaches about giving through this structure shows that generosity was always central.

What the Bible Teaches About Giving: Jesus's Teaching

What the Bible teaches about giving transforms in the New Testament. Rather than emphasizing laws and percentages, Jesus elevated giving to a matter of heart transformation.

Matthew 19:21 records what the Bible teaches about giving through Jesus's encounter with the rich young ruler: "If you want to be perfect, go, sell your possessions and give to the poor, and you will have treasure in heaven." While not a universal command, what the Bible teaches about giving reveals Jesus's radical view of discipleship.

Luke 12:33 captures what the Bible teaches about giving through Jesus's perspective: "Sell your possessions and give to the poor. Provide purses for yourselves that will not wear out, a treasure in heaven that will never fail." What the Bible teaches about giving prioritizes heavenly investment over earthly accumulation.

A Christian's guide to giving recognizes that Jesus emphasized motivation over mechanics. What the Bible teaches about giving focuses on the heart condition rather than prescribed percentages.

What the Bible Teaches About Giving: Paul's Guidance

Paul developed comprehensive teaching about what the Bible teaches about giving. Second Corinthians 8:1-2 celebrates what the Bible teaches about giving through the Macedonian churches: "In the midst of a very severe trial, their overflowing joy and their extreme poverty welled up in rich generosity."

What the Bible teaches about giving in 2 Corinthians 9:6 uses agricultural principle: "Whoever sows sparingly will reap sparingly, and whoever sows generously will reap generously." A Christian's guide to giving recognizes this as spiritual law.

First Corinthians 16:1-4 provides what the Bible teaches about giving practically: "On the first day of every week, each one of you should set aside a sum of money in keeping with your income." What the Bible teaches about giving includes regular, proportional, planned giving.

A Christian's guide to giving values Paul's emphasis that what the Bible teaches about giving flows from grace, not obligation. Second Corinthians 8:12 explains what the Bible teaches about giving: "For if the willingness is there, the gift is acceptable according to what one has, not according to what one does not have."

What the Bible Teaches About Giving: Trust and Provision

What the Bible teaches about giving is grounded in trust. Matthew 6:25-34 records Jesus's teaching addressing worry: "Do not worry about your life, what you will eat or drink... But seek first his kingdom and his righteousness, and all these things will be given to you as well."

A Christian's guide to giving teaches that generosity flows from believing God will provide. Philippians 4:19 promises what the Bible teaches about giving: "And my God will meet all your needs according to the riches of his glory in Christ Jesus."

Luke 6:38 captures what the Bible teaches about giving through divine provision: "Give, and it will be given to you. A good measure, pressed down, shaken together and running over, will be poured into your lap." A Christian's guide to giving presents giving as investment with return.

What the Bible Teaches About Giving: Justice and Compassion

A crucial element of what the Bible teaches about giving involves justice. God's concern for vulnerable populations runs throughout Scripture.

Deuteronomy 15:10-11 expresses what the Bible teaches about giving: "Give generously to him and do so without a grudging heart... There will always be poor people in the land. Therefore I command you to be openhanded toward your fellow Israelites who are poor and needy."

First John 3:17 makes what the Bible teaches about giving clear: "If anyone has material possessions and sees a brother or sister in need but has no pity on them, how can the love of God be in that person?" A Christian's guide to giving connects generosity to justice.

Matthew 25:31-46 describes what the Bible teaches about giving as foundational to judgment itself. Jesus evaluated people based on how they cared for the hungry, thirsty, stranger, naked, and sick. What the Bible teaches about giving reveals this as central to Christian faith.

What the Bible Teaches About Giving: Practical Application

A Christian's guide to giving includes practical steps. What the Bible teaches about giving suggests:

Begin with prayer: Ask God to guide your giving decisions and reveal where He wants you to invest resources.

Give proportionally: What the Bible teaches about giving emphasizes percentages based on capacity, not absolute amounts. As income increases, giving can grow.

Research carefully: What the Bible teaches about giving includes wisdom. Investigate organizations before giving to ensure alignment with your values.

Plan regularly: First Corinthians 16:1-2 suggests regular, planned giving rather than sporadic impulses.

Give multiple ways: What the Bible teaches about giving includes supporting your church, caring for the poor, advancing missions, and supporting God's workers.

FAQ

Q: What does the Bible teach about giving—should I tithe? A: The Bible teaches tithing in the Old Testament. The New Testament doesn't explicitly require it but emphasizes proportional, joyful giving. Many Christians find tithing helpful; others give differently based on conviction and capacity.

Q: What does the Bible teach about giving if I can't afford much? A: The Bible teaches that God values sacrifice and willingness more than amount. The widow's two coins illustrate this. Give what you can with joy; your willingness itself is the gift God values.

Q: What does the Bible teach about giving to secular organizations? A: The Bible teaches that giving should support God's kingdom, care for the poor, and advance justice. You can support secular organizations working toward these goals, though giving to your church and Christian causes should remain primary.

Q: What does the Bible teach about giving if I receive criticism for my giving choices? A: The Bible teaches that your giving is between you and God. Matthew 6:1-4 warns against giving for recognition. Don't be pressured into giving decisions that violate your conscience.

Q: What does the Bible teach about giving in response to financial hardship? A: The Bible teaches God's provision and care. While struggling, continue giving proportionally if possible. The Bible teaches that faithfulness in difficulty deepens trust and invites God's blessing.

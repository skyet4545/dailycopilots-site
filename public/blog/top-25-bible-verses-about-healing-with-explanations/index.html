Top 25 Bible Verses About Healing (With Explanations)

By Bible Copilot Updated July 3, 2026 Scripture-cited · editorial standards
Quick answer

Explore the top 25 Bible verses about healing with detailed explanations. Scripture passages covering physical, emotional, and spiritual restoration.

Introduction to Healing Verses

Bible verses about healing form a substantial thread throughout Scripture, revealing God's compassionate nature and restorative power. These Bible verses about healing provide comfort during illness, hope in suffering, and guidance for those seeking wholeness. Understanding Bible verses about healing requires exploring context and applying their wisdom to contemporary circumstances.

The following collection represents significant Bible verses about healing across both testaments. Each of these Bible verses about healing addresses different dimensions of restoration—physical recovery, emotional healing, spiritual transformation, and relational reconciliation.

The Core 25 Bible Verses About Healing

1. Exodus 15:26 - "I am the LORD, who heals you." This foundational verse establishes God's identity as healer. These Bible verses about healing begin with this declaration of God's essential nature, not as one attribute but as fundamental to who He is.

2. Psalm 30:2 - "LORD my God, I called to you for help, and you healed me." This verse demonstrates personal experience of healing. Bible verses about healing like this one acknowledge both human need and divine response.

3. Psalm 103:2-3 - "Praise the LORD, my soul, and forget not all his benefits—who forgives all your sins and heals all your diseases." These Bible verses about healing connect forgiveness and physical healing, suggesting spiritual wholeness supports physical restoration.

4. Isaiah 53:4-5 - "Surely he took up our pain and bore our suffering... and by his wounds we are healed." These Bible verses about healing identify Christ's suffering as redemptive, establishing that healing flows through Christ's substitutionary work.

5. Jeremiah 30:17 - "But I will restore you to health and heal your wounds, declares the LORD." This verse promises restoration despite current brokenness. Bible verses about healing like this one provide hope during despair.

6. Matthew 4:23 - "Jesus went throughout Galilee, teaching in their synagogues, proclaiming the good news of the kingdom, and healing every disease and sickness among the people." These Bible verses about healing demonstrate that healing constituted integral to gospel proclamation in Jesus's ministry.

7. Matthew 8:16-17 - "When evening came, many who were demon-possessed were brought to him, and he drove out the spirits with a word and healed all the sick. This was to fulfill what was spoken through the prophet Isaiah: 'He took up our infirmities and bore our diseases.'" Bible verses about healing here explicitly connect Christ's work to Old Testament prophecy, showing healing's redemptive significance.

8. Mark 1:40-41 - "A man with leprosy came to him and begged him on his knees, 'If you are willing, can make me clean.' Jesus was indignant." These Bible verses about healing show Jesus's compassion for the most marginalized. Leprosy created both physical and social isolation; healing restored both.

9. Mark 5:34 - "He said to her, 'Daughter, your faith has healed you. Go in peace and be freed from your suffering.'" Bible verses about healing here emphasize the connection between faith and healing, though Jesus himself declared the healing effective.

10. Luke 4:40 - "At sunset, the people brought to Jesus all who had various kinds of sickness, and laying his hands on each one, he healed them." These Bible verses about healing emphasize Jesus's compassion and power to heal diverse conditions.

11. John 11:25-26 - "Jesus said to her, 'I am the resurrection and the life. The one who believes in me will live, even though they die. And whoever lives by believing in me will never die. Do you believe this?'" Bible verses about healing here establish ultimate healing through resurrection, suggesting temporal healing points toward eternal restoration.

12. Acts 3:6-7 - "Then Peter said, 'Silver or gold I do not have, but what I do have I give you. In the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, walk.' Taking him by the right hand, he helped him up, and instantly the man's feet and ankles became strong." These Bible verses about healing demonstrate the apostles' continued healing ministry empowered by the Spirit.

13. Romans 8:26-27 - "In the same way, the Spirit helps us in our weakness. We do not know what we ought to pray for, but the Spirit himself intercedes for us with wordless groans." Bible verses about healing here acknowledge that sometimes healing's pathway remains unclear, yet the Spirit supports us.

14. 2 Corinthians 1:3-4 - "Praise be to the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of compassion and the God of all comfort, who comforts us in all our troubles, so that we can comfort those who are in any trouble with the comfort ourselves receive from God." These Bible verses about healing connect personal healing to capacity for comforting others, suggesting healing serves missional purposes.

15. 2 Corinthians 12:9 - "But he said to me, 'My grace is sufficient for you, for my power is made perfect in weakness.'" Bible verses about healing here teach that sometimes healing involves grace to endure suffering rather than its elimination.

16. 2 Corinthians 13:4 - "For to be sure, Christ was crucified in weakness, yet he lives by God's power. Likewise, we are weak in him, yet by God's power we will live with him to serve you." These Bible verses about healing connect Christ's resurrection power to believers' ongoing empowerment.

17. Philippians 4:6-7 - "Do not be anxious about anything, but in every situation, by prayer and petition, with thanksgiving, present your requests to God. And the peace of God, which transcends all understanding, will guard your hearts and your minds in Christ Jesus." Bible verses about healing here teach that prayer combined with thanksgiving facilitates peace supporting healing.

18. 1 Peter 2:24 - "He himself bore our sins in his body on the cross, so that we might die to sins and live for righteousness; by his wounds you have been healed." These Bible verses about healing establish that Christ's sacrifice provides the foundation for all healing.

19. 3 John 1:2 - "Dear friend, I pray that you may enjoy good health and that all may go well with you, even as your soul is getting along well." Bible verses about healing here connect physical health to spiritual wholeness, showing that comprehensive wellness matters.

20. James 5:14-15 - "Is anyone among you ill? Let them call the elders of the church to pray over them and anoint them with oil in the name of the Lord. And the prayer offered in faith will make the sick person well; the Lord will raise them up." These Bible verses about healing provide explicit instruction for community-based healing ministry.

21. Proverbs 17:22 - "A cheerful heart is good medicine, but a crushed spirit dries up the bones." Bible verses about healing here acknowledge the mind-body connection, that emotional state influences physical health.

22. Psalm 147:3 - "He heals the brokenhearted and binds up their wounds." These Bible verses about healing address emotional and spiritual wounds, recognizing that all dimensions require healing.

23. Nahum 3:19 - "Nothing can heal you; your wound is fatal." Bible verses about healing sometimes describe conditions beyond human remedy, establishing God's unique power to restore.

24. Matthew 9:22 - "Jesus turned and saw her. 'Take heart, daughter,' he said, 'your faith has healed you.' And the woman was healed at that moment." Bible verses about healing here show immediate response to faith, emphasizing faith's important but not determinative role.

25. Luke 6:19 - "And the people all tried to touch him, because power was coming from him and healing them all." These concluding Bible verses about healing emphasize that healing flows from Christ's presence and power.

FAQ

Q: Are these Bible verses about healing promises that apply universally to all believers? A: Bible verses about healing contain promises of God's power and willingness to restore, yet Scripture doesn't promise universal physical healing in the present age. These verses should be understood within Scripture's larger teaching about suffering and redemption.

Q: How should I interpret Bible verses about healing if I remain ill despite prayer? A: Bible verses about healing, when combined with passages about suffering and grace, teach that God sometimes withholds immediate healing for greater purposes. Illness doesn't indicate divine displeasure or lack of faith. God promises presence, not always healing according to human timeline.

Q: Do these Bible verses about healing support faith-healing exclusively? A: No. Bible verses about healing, combined with Proverbs's affirmation of doctors and medical wisdom, support both divine healing and medical treatment. These verses should never become justification for rejecting medical care.

Q: Which Bible verses about healing emphasize community? A: James 5:14-15 most explicitly addresses community healing practice. Many other verses show believers praying for one another. Bible verses about healing emphasize that isolation hinders recovery while community facilitates it.

Q: How do Bible verses about healing address emotional and spiritual restoration? A: Psalm 147:3, 2 Corinthians 1:3-4, and others address emotional healing. Bible verses about healing recognize that emotional and spiritual wounds require restoration just as physical injuries do. Comprehensive healing involves all dimensions of personhood.

What Does Proverbs 31:25 Mean? "Clothed With Strength"

📖 Bible Copilot ⏱ ~3 min read

Short answer: Proverbs 31:25 praises a woman whose inner character gives her confidence about what lies ahead: "Strength and dignity are her clothing. She laughs at the time to come" (WEB); the KJV reads, "Strength and honour are her clothing; and she shall rejoice in time to come." "Clothed" with strength and dignity means these qualities visibly characterize her life, and because of them she faces the future without fear — she can even "laugh" at it, secure in God-rooted character rather than anxious about tomorrow.

The context

Proverbs 31:10–31 is an acrostic poem (each verse begins with a successive letter of the Hebrew alphabet) describing "a worthy woman" or "a wife of noble character." It closes the book of Proverbs as a portrait of wisdom lived out in everyday life — in work, generosity, family, and business. Importantly, verse 30 gives the key to the whole poem: "Charm is deceitful, and beauty is vain; but a woman who fears the LORD, she shall be praised." So her strength and fearless confidence in verse 25 are not self-made bravado; they flow from her reverence for God and the diligent, faithful life that reverence produces.

What it means, phrase by phrase

  • "Strength and dignity are her clothing" — "Clothing" is a metaphor: strength (inner and moral firmness) and dignity/honor characterize her the way garments characterize a person. They are what people see when they look at her life.
  • "She laughs at the time to come / she shall rejoice in time to come" — To "laugh at the future" is to face it without dread. She is not anxious about tomorrow because her life is built on God and lived with diligence. The laughter is confidence, not carelessness.

This is not a claim that she never faces hardship; the poem shows her working hard and planning wisely (she "considers a field and buys it," v. 16). Her confidence is grounded in character and trust in God, which steady her against fear of the unknown.

Cross-references

  • Proverbs 31:30 — the fear of the LORD is what makes her praiseworthy.
  • 1 Peter 3:3–4 — true beauty is "the hidden person of the heart... a gentle and quiet spirit."
  • Proverbs 3:25–26 — "Don't be afraid of sudden fear... for the LORD will be your confidence."
  • Matthew 6:34 — "Therefore don't be anxious for tomorrow."

How to apply it today

Proverbs 31:25 offers a model of confidence that is not rooted in appearance, wealth, or control, but in godly character. For anyone anxious about the future, it points to the source of true security: reverence for God and a faithful, diligent life. "Clothing" yourself with strength and dignity is a daily, cumulative thing — built through wise choices, integrity, and trust in God over time. The result is the freedom to face an uncertain future with peace rather than dread. Though the passage describes a woman, its underlying wisdom — that character and the fear of the LORD produce fearless confidence — applies to every reader.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does it mean to be "clothed with strength and dignity"? It is a metaphor: strength and dignity characterize her life the way clothing characterizes a person's appearance. These inner qualities are what stand out about her, worn consistently rather than put on for show.

Why does she "laugh at the future"? Because she faces what is coming without fear. Her confidence comes from her reverence for God (Proverbs 31:30) and a diligent, faithful life, which free her from anxiety about tomorrow. The laughter expresses secure trust, not carelessness.

Is Proverbs 31 only for women? The passage specifically portrays a "woman of noble character," and it is often read at weddings and as an ideal for godly women. But its core wisdom — that the fear of the LORD produces strength, dignity, and fearless confidence — is instructive for every reader.

Does this verse mean a godly life will be free of hardship? No. The poem shows her working hard and planning carefully, implying real challenges. Her fearlessness is not the absence of difficulty but confidence rooted in character and trust in God as she faces the future.

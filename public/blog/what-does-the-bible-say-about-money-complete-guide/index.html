What Does the Bible Say About Money? (Complete Guide)

By Bible Copilot Updated July 3, 2026 Scripture-cited · editorial standards
Quick answer

What does the Bible say about money? Get a complete guide with verses on wealth, generosity, stewardship, and financial wisdom for Christians.

Introduction

What does the Bible say about money? This comprehensive question deserves a comprehensive answer. What does the Bible say about money spans principles of stewardship, warnings about greed, commands to help the poor, and guidance on earning, spending, and saving. Understanding what the Bible says about money requires examining multiple passages and themes.

What does the Bible say about money isn't one simple answer but an integrated perspective addressing money's practical and spiritual dimensions. What does the Bible say about money teaches both Old Testament law and New Testament transformation. This complete guide explores the full biblical perspective.

What the Bible Says About Money's Ultimate Owner

What does the Bible say about money starts with ownership. Psalm 24:1 establishes the foundation: "The earth is the Lord's, and everything in it, the world, and all who live in it." What the Bible says about money is that God is the ultimate owner of all resources.

This principle radically reframes your relationship with money. What does the Bible say about money means you're a manager, not an owner. What the Bible says about money teaches that you will answer to God for how you managed His resources. This shifts financial stewardship from selfish optimization to faithful responsibility.

What the Bible Says About Money and Work

What does the Bible say about money and work? Genesis 2:15 shows that work existed before sin, as part of God's good design. Proverbs 10:4 states, "Lazy hands make for poverty, but diligent hands bring wealth." What the Bible says about money affirms that honest work is virtuous and produces provision.

What the Bible says about money includes 2 Thessalonians 3:10: "The one who is unwilling to work shall not eat." What the Bible says about money celebrates self-sufficiency through work. However, what the Bible says about money also demands honesty. Proverbs 10:2 warns, "Ill-gotten treasures bring no benefit, but righteousness delivers from death."

What the Bible Says About Money and Greed

What does the Bible say about money regarding greed? The frequently misquoted 1 Timothy 6:10 states, "For the love of money is a root of all kinds of evil." What the Bible says about money distinguishes between having money and loving money. Money itself isn't evil; obsessive pursuit of it is.

What the Bible says about money through the parable of the rich fool (Luke 12:15-21) shows a man who accumulated increasingly without regard for God or others. He dies suddenly, his wealth useless. What the Bible says about money teaches that trusting in wealth is misplaced faith.

What the Bible Says About Money and Generosity

What does the Bible say about money regarding giving? Jesus's words in Acts 20:35 declare, "It is more blessed to give than to receive." What the Bible says about money teaches that generosity produces greater blessing than accumulation.

2 Corinthians 9:6-7 explains, "Whoever sows sparingly will also reap sparingly, and whoever sows generously will also reap generously. Each of you should give what you have decided in your heart to give, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver." What the Bible says about money emphasizes joyful, voluntary generosity from grateful hearts.

What the Bible Says About Money and Debt

What does the Bible say about money regarding borrowing? Proverbs 22:7 warns, "The rich rule over the poor, and the borrower is servant to the lender." What the Bible says about money presents debt as a burden that limits freedom and creates vulnerability.

What the Bible says about money isn't an absolute prohibition but a caution. Romans 13:8 advises, "Let no debt remain outstanding, except the continuing debt to love one another." What the Bible says about money encourages moving toward financial freedom from debt obligation.

What the Bible Says About Money and Care for the Poor

What does the Bible say about money regarding the vulnerable? Proverbs 31:8-9 commands, "Speak up for those who cannot speak for themselves, for the rights of all who are destitute. Speak up and judge fairly; defend the rights of the poor and needy."

What the Bible says about money includes repeated emphasis on caring for those in poverty. Proverbs 14:31 declares, "Whoever oppresses the poor shows contempt for their Maker, but whoever is kind to the needy honors God." What the Bible says about money teaches that kindness to the poor is honoring to God.

What the Bible Says About Money and Planning

What does the Bible say about money regarding preparation for the future? Proverbs 21:5 states, "The plans of the diligent lead to profit as surely as haste leads to poverty." What the Bible says about money celebrates thoughtful planning and foresight.

What the Bible says about money affirms saving for future needs, emergency preparation, and long-term thinking. Proverbs 27:12 teaches, "The prudent see danger and take refuge, but the simple keep going and pay the penalty." What the Bible says about money values wisdom that anticipates and prepares.

What the Bible Says About Money and Trust

What does the Bible say about money regarding faith? Matthew 6:25-34 contains Jesus's most extended teaching about financial anxiety. He concludes, "But seek first his kingdom and his righteousness, and all these things will be given to you as well."

What the Bible says about money isn't that you shouldn't work or plan but that you shouldn't worry obsessively. What the Bible says about money teaches radical trust: "And my God will meet all your needs according to the riches of his glory in Christ Jesus" (Philippians 4:19).

What the Bible Says About Money and Contentment

What does the Bible say about money regarding satisfaction? 1 Timothy 6:6 states, "But godliness with contentment is great gain." What the Bible says about money teaches that contentment produces greater wealth than accumulation.

What the Bible says about money echoes in 1 Timothy 6:8: "But if we have food and clothing, we will be content with that." What the Bible says about money suggests that wanting fewer things produces more happiness than having many things.

What the Bible Says About Money and Idolatry

What does the Bible say about money as a spiritual issue? Matthew 6:24 presents mammon (money/wealth) as a competing deity: "No one can serve two masters. Either you will hate the one and love the other, or you will be devoted to the one and despise the other. You cannot serve both God and money."

What the Bible says about money recognizes that money can displace God as your ultimate concern. What the Bible says about money teaches that trust, hope, and allegiance belong to God alone, not to financial security.

FAQ

Q: Does the Bible say I should be poor? A: No. Many biblical figures were wealthy and godly. What the Bible says about money condemns greed and idolatry, not wealth itself. The issue is your heart's orientation, not your bank account.

Q: What does the Bible say about taking out student loans? A: Student loans are not explicitly addressed, but biblical principles apply: avoid unnecessary debt, pursue wisdom in educational choices, and work toward repayment. What the Bible says about money affirms education's value but warns against crushing debt.

Q: Does the Bible say wealthy people can't follow Jesus? A: Jesus's statement that "it is easier for a camel to go through the eye of a needle than for someone who is rich to enter the kingdom of God" reveals that wealth is a spiritual danger, not an absolute barrier. What the Bible says about money calls the wealthy to particular vigilance.

Q: What does the Bible say about giving 10 percent (tithing)? A: Tithing was an Old Testament practice. The New Testament emphasizes joyful, generous giving according to what you've decided. What the Bible says about money encourages generous giving motivated by grace, not obligation.

Q: How does what the Bible says about money apply to corporate employment? A: What the Bible says about money includes working honestly and diligently. However, if your job requires dishonesty or unethical behavior, what the Bible says about money would counsel finding employment that aligns with your values.

Revenge in the Bible: What Every Christian Should Know

By Bible Copilot Updated July 3, 2026 Scripture-cited · editorial standards
Quick answer

Essential guide to understanding revenge in the Bible. Learn what Scripture teaches and how Christians should respond to the desire for retaliation.

If you're a Christian dealing with the desire for revenge, understanding what the Bible teaches about retaliation is essential for your spiritual health.

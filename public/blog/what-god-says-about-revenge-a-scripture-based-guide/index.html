What God Says About Revenge: A Scripture-Based Guide

By Bible Copilot Updated July 3, 2026 Scripture-cited · editorial standards
Quick answer

Learn what God says about revenge through Scripture. Discover biblical principles for overcoming retaliation and finding peace through God's justice.

What God says about revenge addresses one of humanity's deepest struggles—the desire to punish those who hurt us. Throughout Scripture, what God says about revenge reveals His concern for justice, healing, and our spiritual wellbeing. Rather than treating revenge as a minor moral issue, what God says about revenge shows that God recognizes this temptation as serious and worthy of His direct instruction. This Scripture-based guide will show you precisely what God says about revenge and how these teachings can transform your response to injustice.

What God Says About Revenge: It's His Responsibility, Not Yours

Romans 12:19 is foundational to what God says about revenge: "Do not take revenge, my dear friends, but leave room for God's wrath, for it is written: 'It is mine to avenge; I will repay,' says the Lord." What God says about revenge begins with a clear reassignment of responsibility.

God is saying: this is My job, not yours. What God says about revenge liberates us from the impossible task of being our own avenger. What God says about revenge allows us to release the burden and focus on our own healing instead.

What God Says About Revenge: Wait for His Action

Proverbs 20:22 shows what God says about revenge about the proper response: "Do not say, 'I'll pay you back for this wrong!' Wait for the Lord, and he will avenge you." What God says about revenge includes instruction about patience.

What God says about revenge teaches that waiting for God's justice is the wise response. What God says about revenge recognizes that waiting is difficult, but it's infinitely better than taking justice into our own hands.

What God Says About Revenge: Christ Demonstrated It

1 Peter 2:23 reveals what God says about revenge through Christ's example: "When they hurled their insults at him, he did not retaliate; when he suffered, he made no threats. Instead, he entrusted himself to him who judges justly." What God says about revenge is embodied in Jesus.

What God says about revenge shows that Christ, when faced with extreme injustice, chose not to retaliate. What God says about revenge teaches that if Jesus could refrain from revenge, so can we—with His help.

What God Says About Revenge: Choose Good Instead

Romans 12:21 demonstrates what God says about revenge in terms of alternatives: "Do not be overcome by evil, but overcome evil with good." What God says about revenge offers a positive alternative to retaliation.

What God says about revenge isn't just "don't seek revenge"—it includes actively choosing good. What God says about revenge shows that responding with kindness actually breaks revenge cycles.

What God Says About Revenge: Turn It Into Prayer

Matthew 5:44 shows what God says about revenge about the mechanism for release: "Love your enemies and pray for those who persecute you." What God says about revenge includes transforming your response through prayer.

What God says about revenge teaches that praying for someone's good is incompatible with seeking their harm. What God says about revenge provides prayer as the means by which you release your desire for revenge.

What God Says About Revenge: Don't Repay Evil for Evil

1 Thessalonians 5:15 reinforces what God says about revenge: "Make sure that nobody pays back wrong for wrong, but always try to be kind to each other and to everyone else." What God says about revenge explicitly prohibits retaliatory behavior.

What God says about revenge calls for kindness even in response to wrong. What God says about revenge shows that this is possible—it's a choice, not impossible.

What God Says About Revenge: The Wise Avoid It

Proverbs 27:12 demonstrates what God says about revenge about wisdom: "The prudent see danger and take refuge, but the simple keep going and pay the penalty." What God says about revenge associates wisdom with avoiding retaliation.

What God says about revenge suggests that those who recognize revenge's danger will protect themselves by releasing grudges. What God says about revenge shows that simple people stumble into revenge and pay the consequences.

What God Says About Revenge: God's Justice Is Sure

Hebrews 10:30 reassures what God says about revenge about outcome: "For we know him who said, 'It is mine to avenge; I will repay.'" What God says about revenge guarantees that justice will happen.

What God says about revenge allows us to release our need for revenge because we trust God's promise. What God says about revenge makes clear that wrongdoing won't be overlooked.

What God Says About Revenge: It's Connected to Pride

Proverbs 13:10 reveals what God says about revenge about its roots: "Where there is strife, there is pride, but those who take advice are wise." What God says about revenge shows that revenge desires often stem from pride.

What God says about revenge suggests that humble people who take wise counsel avoid retaliation. What God says about revenge identifies pride as what fuels the desire to punish others.

Q: What does God say about revenge if I've been seriously wronged? A: What God says about revenge acknowledges real harm while calling for healing through God's grace, which is more powerful than the harm.

Q: How does what God says about revenge help me release my anger? A: What God says about revenge invites you to pray for the person, trust God's justice, and gradually release the burden through these spiritual practices.

Q: What's the core of what God says about revenge? A: What God says about revenge fundamentally says: release this to Me. I'll handle it. You focus on your healing and forgiveness. Trust Me with justice.

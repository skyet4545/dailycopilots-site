What God Says About Confidence: A Scripture-Based Guide

By Bible Copilot Updated July 3, 2026 Scripture-cited · editorial standards
Quick answer

Discover what God says about confidence directly from Scripture. Learn biblical truths about trust and courage from God's Word.

When you want to understand confidence, the best approach is to see what God Himself says about it. Rather than relying on modern psychology or self-help wisdom, this Scripture-based guide focuses exclusively on what God says about confidence through His Word. You'll discover that God doesn't just permit confidence—He actively speaks it into His people's lives, commanding and encouraging it throughout Scripture.

God Says: I Am With You

One of the most repeated themes in what God says about confidence is the promise of His presence. This is God's foundational answer to fear and uncertainty.

Joshua 1:9 - "Have I not commanded you? Be strong and courageous. Do not be afraid; do not be discouraged, for the Lord your God will be with you wherever you go." (NIV)

Notice what God says directly: He commands you to be courageous. Why? Because He promises His presence. God doesn't ask you to muster up confidence alone; He promises to be with you. This transforms confidence from something you create to something you recognize based on God's promise.

Deuteronomy 31:8 - "The Lord himself goes before you and will be with you; he will never leave you nor forsake you. Do not be afraid; do not be discouraged." (NIV)

God explicitly says He goes before you and stays with you. He tells you not to be afraid because His presence is assured. What God says about confidence includes this assurance of His presence in every situation.

God Says: I Am Your Strength

When you're weak, God says something powerful about where your confidence should come from.

Psalm 27:1 - "The Lord is my light and my salvation—whom shall I fear? The Lord is the stronghold of my life—of whom shall I be afraid?" (NIV)

God positions Himself as your stronghold. When God says this, He's promising to be your protection and strength. Your confidence can be complete because your strength comes from outside yourself, from an infinite source.

Isaiah 40:29 - "He gives strength to the weary and increases the power of the weak." (NIV)

What God specifically says about confidence is that when you're exhausted and weak, He provides strength and power. You don't have to fake it or push through on your own. God gives strength actively and generously.

2 Corinthians 12:9 - "But he said to me, 'My grace is sufficient for you, for my power is made perfect in weakness.'" (NIV)

God says something counterintuitive: His power works best in your weakness. When you admit your weakness instead of pretending to have strength, you open yourself to God's power. This shifts how you think about confidence entirely.

God Says: Trust Me Completely

God speaks clearly about the kind of trust He desires—and the confidence that comes from it.

Proverbs 3:5-6 - "Trust in the Lord with all your heart and lean not on your own understanding; in all your ways submit to him, and he will make your paths straight." (NIV)

God says: trust with all your heart, not partially or conditionally. Don't rely on your own understanding, which is limited. Submit to Him completely. When you do, God promises to make your paths straight. This is what God says creates the kind of confidence that leads to a clear life direction.

Psalm 37:5-6 - "Commit your way to the Lord; trust in him and he will do this: He will make your righteous reward shine like the dawn, your vindication like the midday sun." (NIV)

God says that when you commit your way to Him and trust, He will vindicate you and reward you. You don't have to fight for justice yourself or worry about whether you'll be treated fairly. God promises to handle it.

God Says: Your Fear Is Not From Me

A powerful thing God says about confidence is that debilitating fear doesn't come from Him.

2 Timothy 1:7 - "For the Spirit God gave us does not make us timid, but gives us power, love and a sound mind." (NIV)

God explicitly says that timidity isn't from Him. When you experience paralyzing fear, you're not listening to God's voice. God's voice produces power, love, and sound thinking. If fear is controlling you, it's not coming from God.

1 John 4:18 - "There is no fear in love. But perfect love drives out fear, because fear has to do with punishment. The one who fears is not made perfect in love." (NIV)

God says that perfect love drives out fear. As you grasp how completely God loves you—how you'll never face punishment because Christ paid for it—fear loses its hold. What God says about confidence includes this revolutionary idea: His love makes fear powerless.

God Says: I Hear Your Prayers

A specific promise God makes is about your prayer life and His responsiveness to it.

1 John 5:14-15 - "This is the confidence we have in approaching God: that if we ask anything according to his will, he hears us. And if we know that he hears us—whatever we ask—we know that we have what we asked of him." (NIV)

What God says directly is: I hear your prayers. Not sometimes or if you're good enough, but genuinely. And I respond. God promises that your prayers matter and produce results. This is revolutionary for confidence in your prayer life.

Psalm 27:8 - "My heart says of you, 'Seek his face!' Your face, Lord, I will seek." (NIV)

God says—through the psalmist—"Seek my face." This is an invitation. God isn't hiding or distant. He's inviting you to seek Him. What God says about confidence includes His invitation to relationship.

God Says: You Are My Child

Perhaps the most foundational thing God says about confidence is about your identity and relationship with Him.

1 John 3:1 - "See what great love the Father has lavished on us, that we should be called children of God! And that is what we are!" (NIV)

What God says about you is remarkable: you're His child. He lavishes love on you. This isn't conditional on performance; this is your status. God says this is what you are—not what you're trying to become, but what you already are.

Romans 8:15-16 - "The Spirit you received does not make you slaves, so that you live in fear again; rather, the Spirit you received brought about your adoption to sonship. And by him we cry, 'Abba, Father.' The Spirit himself testifies with our spirit that we are God's children." (NIV)

God says you're adopted into His family. You're not a slave; you're His child. You can call Him "Father" (Abba). The Holy Spirit confirms this reality within you. What God says about your identity is settled and secure.

God Says: I Have Plans for You

God speaks specifically about having purpose and direction for your life.

Jeremiah 29:11 - "For I know the plans I have for you, declares the Lord, 'plans to prosper you and not to harm you, plans to give you hope and a future.'" (NIV)

God says directly: I have plans for you. These plans are for prosperity, not harm. They're designed to give you hope and a future. What God says dismisses the fear that your life is random or purposeless.

Ephesians 2:10 - "For we are God's handiwork, created in Christ Jesus to do good works, which God prepared in advance for us to do." (NIV)

God says you're His handiwork—His masterpiece. The good works you do aren't random; they're prepared in advance. You're not a mistake or an afterthought. What God says about your creation is that it was intentional and purposeful.

God Says: I Work Everything for Good

In difficult circumstances, what does God say? He addresses suffering and hardship directly.

Romans 8:28 - "And we know that in all things God works for the good of those who have loved him, who have been called according to his purpose." (NIV)

God says that even in bad circumstances, He's working for your good. This doesn't mean bad things become good, but that God can work even bad things into His good purposes. What God says about hardship is that it's not the final word; His purpose prevails.

James 1:2-3 - "Consider it pure joy, my brothers and sisters, whenever you face trials of many kinds, because you know that the testing of your faith produces perseverance." (NIV)

God says trials produce perseverance. You can approach difficulty with confidence because you know it's producing something valuable. What God says about suffering is that it serves a purpose in your development.

God Says: My Word Is Reliable

Finally, God speaks about the trustworthiness of His Word.

Proverbs 30:5 - "Every word of God is flawless; he is a shield to those who take refuge in him." (NIV)

God says His Word is flawless. Not sometimes accurate, but always flawless. When you build your confidence on God's Word, you're building on something absolutely reliable. What God says about His Word is that it's a shield—it protects you.

FAQ

Q: Does God say I should ever have doubts? A: God doesn't forbid doubts; He invites you to bring them to Him. What God says is that you can move through doubt into faith through seeking Him.

Q: Does God say what He says about confidence applies to everyone? A: Yes. God's promises are to "those who love him" and "those who are called according to his purpose." If you're His child through Christ, these promises apply to you.

Q: What does God say about confidence when circumstances suggest I shouldn't be confident? A: God consistently says to trust Him regardless of circumstances. He promises His presence and purpose even in dark times, which is the basis for confidence.

Q: Does God say confidence means I'll never face difficulty? A: No. God says He'll be with you through difficulty and work it for good. Confidence isn't about avoiding hardship; it's about trusting through it.

Q: What does God say I should do if I'm struggling to believe what He says about confidence? A: Spend time in His Word, pray honestly about your doubts, and connect with community. Faith grows as you practice these disciplines.

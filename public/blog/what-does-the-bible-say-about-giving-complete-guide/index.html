What Does the Bible Say About Giving? (Complete Guide)

By Bible Copilot Updated July 3, 2026 Scripture-cited · editorial standards
Quick answer

Find answers to what the Bible says about giving. This complete guide covers Scripture's teaching on generosity, motivation, and practical application for Christian living.

Introduction

What does the Bible say about giving? The answer is both simple and profound. Scripture teaches that giving is central to Christian faith, reflecting God's generous character and our trust in His provision. From foundational principles in the Old Testament to Jesus's radical teaching and Paul's practical guidance, what the Bible says about giving addresses every aspect—motivation, amount, method, and purpose. Rather than presenting giving as burdensome obligation, what the Bible says about giving frames generosity as privilege, worship, and spiritual discipline that transforms both giver and receiver. This complete guide explores what the Bible says about giving across both testaments and how it applies to your life today.

The Core Message: Heart Over Amount

What the Bible says about giving begins with motivation. Second Corinthians 9:7 presents God's fundamental concern: "Each of you should give what you have decided in your heart to give, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver." This verse distills what the Bible says about giving—God values the attitude far more than the amount.

Jesus reinforced this when observing the widow's offering in Mark 12:41-44. While wealthy donors gave large sums from their abundance, the poor widow dropped two small coins worth almost nothing. Yet Jesus said she had given more than all the others. What the Bible says about giving through this account is that sacrifice and sincerity matter infinitely more than monetary value.

What the Bible says about giving also emphasizes that external actions without internal transformation are worthless. First Corinthians 13:3 warns, "If I give all I possess to the poor and give over my body to hardship that I might boast, but do not have love, I gain nothing." This teaches that what the Bible says about giving demands a loving heart as the foundation.

Old Testament Foundations of What the Bible Says About Giving

What the Bible says about giving in the Old Testament establishes clear structures. God commanded three types of giving:

The Tithe: Leviticus 27:30 states, "A tithe of everything from the land, whether grain from the soil or fruit from the trees, belongs to the Lord; it is holy to the Lord." This ten percent contribution supported the Levites, who had no land inheritance, and funded community worship and care for vulnerable populations.

Firstfruits: Exodus 23:16 required offering "the firstfruits of the crops you plant." What the Bible says about giving firstfruits emphasizes that we honor God by offering our best portion first, demonstrating that God's work takes priority.

Freewill Offerings: Beyond mandatory giving, Leviticus 23:37 describes additional voluntary offerings. What the Bible says about giving includes this invitation to exceed minimum requirements based on conviction and abundance.

What the Bible says about giving in the Old Testament served multiple purposes: supporting religious leadership, providing for vulnerable populations, funding community worship, and expressing faith in God's ongoing provision.

Jesus Transforms What the Bible Says About Giving

The New Testament represents a significant shift in what the Bible says about giving. Rather than emphasizing percentages and laws, Jesus elevated giving to a matter of heart transformation.

What the Bible says about giving through Jesus appears most radically in Luke 12:33: "Sell your possessions and give to the poor. Provide purses for yourselves that will not wear out, a treasure in heaven that will never fail." What the Bible says about giving here isn't describing an absolute command for everyone but revealing the heart Jesus values—willingness to sacrifice earthly security for eternal investment.

What the Bible says about giving in Matthew 6:19-21 emphasizes the spiritual principle: "Do not store up for yourselves treasures on earth, where moths and vermin destroy, and where thieves break in and steal. But store up for yourselves treasures in heaven... For where your treasure is, there your heart will be also." What the Bible says about giving ultimately concerns where we place ultimate value.

Jesus also taught that what the Bible says about giving includes caring for the vulnerable. In Matthew 25:31-46, He described the final judgment, where the righteous were those who fed the hungry, gave drink to the thirsty, welcomed strangers, clothed the naked, and cared for the sick. What the Bible says about giving becomes inseparable from justice and compassion.

Paul's Theology of What the Bible Says About Giving

Paul developed a comprehensive theology of what the Bible says about giving in his letters. He taught that generosity flows from grace, not law.

What the Bible says about giving in 2 Corinthians 8:1-2 celebrates the Macedonian churches who gave generously despite "extreme poverty." Paul explains that their generosity "welled up in rich generosity" because of grace, not prosperity. What the Bible says about giving shows that economic status doesn't determine generosity—conviction does.

What the Bible says about giving in 2 Corinthians 9:6 uses agricultural metaphor: "Whoever sows sparingly will reap sparingly, and whoever sows generously will reap generously." Paul teaches that generosity is investment with return. What the Bible says about giving here connects our generosity to divine provision.

What the Bible says about giving in 1 Corinthians 16:1-4 provides practical instruction: "On the first day of every week, each one of you should set aside a sum of money in keeping with your income." Paul acknowledges that giving should be proportional—what the Bible says about giving requires individuals to give according to their capacity.

Generosity and Provision: What the Bible Says About Giving

What the Bible says about giving connects generosity with God's provision. Proverbs 11:24 presents a paradox: "One person gives freely, yet gains even more; another withholds unduly, but comes to poverty." This challenges worldly wisdom about hoarding.

What the Bible says about giving in Malachi 3:10 presents God's dramatic promise: "Bring the whole tithe into the storehouse... and see if I will not throw open the floodgates of heaven and pour out so much blessing that there will not be room enough to store it." What the Bible says about giving here suggests that faithful generosity invites divine blessing.

Luke 6:38 captures this principle: "Give, and it will be given to you. A good measure, pressed down, shaken together and running over, will be poured into your lap." What the Bible says about giving operates on principles of divine generosity that exceed what we might expect.

What the Bible says about giving also emphasizes trust. When Jesus taught the Lord's Prayer, He instructed His disciples to pray for "daily bread" (Matthew 6:11). What the Bible says about giving assumes trust in God's provision, which liberates givers from fear and enables generosity.

What the Bible Says About Giving and Justice

A crucial element of what the Bible says about giving involves justice and care for the vulnerable. Deuteronomy 15:11 acknowledges that "there will always be poor people in the land" and commands believers to be "openhanded toward your fellow Israelites who are poor and needy."

What the Bible says about giving in Proverbs 31:8-9 calls readers to "speak up for those who cannot speak for themselves, for the rights of all who are destitute. Speak up and judge fairly; defend the rights of the poor and needy."

What the Bible says about giving in 1 John 3:17 makes the connection explicit: "If anyone has material possessions and sees a brother or sister in need but has no pity on them, how can the love of God be in that person?" What the Bible says about giving demands that love translate into action on behalf of the vulnerable.

The early church demonstrated this in Acts 2:44-45, where believers "sold property and possessions to give to anyone who had need." What the Bible says about giving produces community where material goods are distributed according to necessity.

Practical Wisdom: What the Bible Says About Giving

Beyond theology, what the Bible says about giving includes practical guidance. Second Corinthians 8:12 reassures struggling givers: "For if the willingness is there, the gift is acceptable according to what one has, not according to what one does not have." What the Bible says about giving accepts proportional offerings from those with limited means.

What the Bible says about giving also involves discernment. Proverbs 10:19 teaches wisdom in decision-making, and what the Bible says about giving should flow from prayerful consideration of where God calls us to invest.

What the Bible says about giving includes sustaining the work of God. Paul describes gifts sent to him as "a fragrant offering, an acceptable sacrifice, pleasing to God" (Philippians 4:18). What the Bible says about giving recognizes that supporting God's workers and mission is an act of worship.

Finally, what the Bible says about giving emphasizes community. Rather than isolated individual acts, giving creates bonds and strengthens the body of Christ. This communal dimension is evident throughout Scripture.

FAQ

Q: What does the Bible say about giving if I'm struggling financially? A: What the Bible says about giving emphasizes willingness and sacrifice over amount. If you have little, give little with joy. As your circumstances improve, your giving can grow. What the Bible says about giving values the attitude far more than the size.

Q: Does what the Bible says about giving require a ten percent tithe? A: What the Bible says about giving through New Testament teaching doesn't explicitly require ten percent. While the Old Testament tithe was mandated, Jesus and Paul emphasize grace-motivated, proportional giving decided by individual conscience.

Q: What does the Bible say about giving to organizations beyond my church? A: What the Bible says about giving includes supporting the poor, missions, and God's kingdom work broadly. Your giving can go to multiple causes aligned with Scripture's priorities—justice, mercy, and the Gospel.

Q: How does what the Bible says about giving apply to modern digital giving and online platforms? A: What the Bible says about giving addresses principles rather than methods. Whether giving through traditional offerings, online platforms, or direct assistance, the same principles apply—joyful, proportional, motivated by love.

Q: What does the Bible say about giving if the recipient misuses the gift? A: What the Bible says about giving emphasizes giving with wisdom and discernment. While we should exercise judgment about where we give, our responsibility is to give generously and ethically. The recipient's use isn't ultimately our accountability.

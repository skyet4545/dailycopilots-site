Top 25 Bible Verses About Sickness (With Explanations)

By Bible Copilot Updated July 3, 2026 Scripture-cited · editorial standards
Quick answer

Explore 25 powerful Bible verses about sickness, healing, and faith. Each verse explained for comfort and spiritual insight during illness.

When facing sickness or caring for someone who is ill, Scripture offers comfort, hope, and wisdom. These 25 Bible verses about sickness address different aspects of illness—from God's compassion and healing power to the spiritual lessons found in suffering. Each verse provides a unique perspective on how to navigate sickness with faith.

Verses About God's Compassion and Comfort

1. Matthew 4:24 - "News about him spread all over Syria, and people brought to him all who were ill with various diseases, those suffering severe pain, the demon-possessed, those having seizures, and the paralyzed; and he healed them all."

This verse demonstrates Jesus's comprehensive approach to sickness. He didn't turn anyone away or question their worthiness of healing. The phrase "healed them all" emphasizes His willingness and power to address every form of illness. This reveals God's heart of compassion toward the suffering.

2. Isaiah 53:4-5 - "Surely he took up our pain and bore our suffering, yet we considered him punished by God, stricken by him, and afflicted. But he was pierced for our transgressions, he was crushed for our iniquities; the punishment that brought us peace was on him, and by his wounds we are healed."

This messianic prophecy connects Christ's suffering to our healing—both physical and spiritual. While not every Christian will experience physical healing, Christ's redemptive work addresses the root cause of all suffering and ultimately leads to complete restoration.

3. 2 Corinthians 1:3-4 - "Praise be to the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of compassion and the God of all comfort, who comforts us in all our troubles, so that we can comfort those who are in any trouble with the comfort ourselves receive from God."

This passage establishes God as fundamentally compassionate and comforting. When sickness brings suffering, believers can confidently approach God knowing His nature is to comfort and care. Moreover, our experience of comfort positions us to comfort others facing similar struggles.

4. Psalm 23:4 - "Even though I walk through the darkest valley, I will fear no evil, for you are with me; your rod and your staff, they comfort me."

Psalm 23 doesn't promise absence of difficulty; it promises God's presence within difficulty. The "darkest valley" includes seasons of sickness and suffering. The comfort comes not from escape but from companionship with the Shepherd who knows and leads us through darkness.

5. Matthew 11:28 - "Come to me, all you who are weary and burdened, and I will give you rest."

Sickness brings weariness—physical, emotional, and spiritual. Jesus invites the tired and heavy-laden to come to Him for rest. This isn't necessarily physical healing but spiritual restoration that sustains us through sickness.

Verses About Seeking Prayer and Community

6. James 5:13-14 - "Is anyone among you in trouble? Let them pray. Is anyone happy? Let them sing songs of praise. Is anyone among you sick? Let them call the elders of the church to pray over them and anoint them with oil in the name of the Lord."

This passage establishes prayer as the primary response to sickness and emphasizes community involvement. Rather than isolating during illness, believers are encouraged to call the church community. This verse about sickness teaches that faith is not solitary but communal.

7. James 5:15-16 - "And the prayer offered in faith will make the sick person well; the Lord will raise them up. If they have sinned, they will be forgiven. Therefore confess your sins to each other and pray for each other so that you may be healed."

This verse about sickness connects prayer, faith, confession, and healing. The healing mentioned encompasses physical, emotional, and spiritual dimensions. Confession and prayer work together to address the whole person.

8. 1 Thessalonians 5:25 - "Brothers and sisters, pray for us too."

Even Paul, a powerful apostle, requested prayer. This verse about sickness reminds us that asking for prayer is not weakness but biblical practice. When sick, request intercession from your church community.

9. Philippians 4:6-7 - "Do not be anxious about anything, but in every situation, by prayer and petition, with thanksgiving, present your requests to God. And the peace of God, which transcends all understanding, will guard your hearts and your minds in Christ Jesus."

Sickness often brings anxiety. This verse encourages transforming anxiety into prayer. Rather than suppressing worry, we're invited to bring it directly to God. The result is supernatural peace that guards us emotionally and mentally during illness.

10. 1 Peter 5:7 - "Cast all your anxiety on him because he cares for you."

This verse about sickness emphasizes that anxiety about illness can be fully surrendered to God. The assurance "he cares for you" provides the foundation for trust. God's care is the basis for releasing our worries.

Verses About Healing and Recovery

11. Mark 1:40-42 - "A man with leprosy came to him and begged him on his knees, 'If you are willing, you can make me clean.' Jesus was indignant. He reached out his hand and touched the man. 'I am willing,' he said. 'Be clean!' Immediately the leprosy left him."

This verse about sickness reveals Jesus's eagerness to heal. His indignation (deep emotion) shows that sickness grieves His heart. The healing was immediate and complete. Touch, compassion, and power characterize Jesus's response to illness.

12. Luke 4:38-39 - "Jesus left the synagogue and went to the home of Simon. Now Simon's mother-in-law was suffering from a high fever, and they asked Jesus to help her. So he bent over her and rebuked the fever, and it left her. She got up at once and began to wait on them."

Even simple fevers received Jesus's attention. This verse about sickness shows that no illness is too minor for God to care about. The healing was complete and instantaneous—she immediately resumed normal activities.

13. Matthew 8:8-9 - "The centurion replied, 'Lord, I do not deserve to have you come under my roof. But just say the word, and my servant will be healed. For I myself am a man under authority, with soldiers under me.'"

This verse about sickness captures the power of faith. The centurion's belief that Jesus's word alone could heal—without needing to visit—demonstrated profound faith. Jesus commended this belief as extraordinary.

14. John 11:40 - "Then Jesus said, 'Did I not tell you that if you believe, you will see the glory of God?'"

Spoken before raising Lazarus from death, this verse about sickness connects faith to witnessing God's power. Belief precedes the display of divine glory in healing.

15. Psalm 107:20 - "He sent out his word and healed them; he rescued them from the grave."

This verse about sickness emphasizes the power of God's word to heal. Throughout Scripture, healing often comes through God's spoken or written word combined with faith.

Verses About Suffering and Spiritual Growth

16. Romans 5:3-4 - "Not only so, but we also glory in our sufferings, because we know that suffering produces perseverance; perseverance, character; and character, hope."

This challenging verse about sickness reframes suffering's purpose. Rather than being purely negative, sickness can develop spiritual qualities when we respond with faith. This doesn't mean we cause our own suffering but that we can gain redemptive value from suffering we encounter.

17. 1 Peter 1:6-7 - "In all this you greatly rejoice, though now for a little while you may have had to suffer grief in all kinds of trials. These have come so that the proven genuineness of your faith—of greater worth than gold, which perishes even though refined by fire—may result in praise, glory and honor when Jesus Christ is revealed."

This verse about sickness compares faith tested through suffering to gold refined by fire. The temporary nature of suffering ("for a little while") contrasts with the eternal value of refined faith.

18. 2 Corinthians 12:9 - "But he said to me, 'My grace is sufficient for you, for my power is made perfect in weakness.'"

Paul received this verse about sickness when praying that God would remove his affliction. Rather than healing the sickness, God offered sustaining grace. This teaches that God's power operates not primarily through our strength but through our weakness.

19. Hebrews 10:36 - "You need to persevere so that when you have done the will of God, you will receive what he has promised."

Perseverance during sickness demonstrates faith. This verse encourages continued trust and obedience even when healing doesn't come quickly.

20. Job 13:15 - "Though he slay me, yet will I hope in him."

Even when facing devastating suffering, Job maintained hope in God's goodness. This verse about sickness demonstrates faith that doesn't depend on outcome but on God's character.

Verses About the Relationship Between Sin and Sickness

21. John 9:2-3 - "His disciples asked him, 'Rabbi, who sinned, this man or his parents, that he was born blind?' 'Neither this man nor his parents sinned,' said Jesus, 'but this happened so that the works of God might be displayed in his life.'"

This crucial verse about sickness directly contradicts the false teaching that sickness always results from sin. Jesus reframed the blindness as an opportunity for God's redemptive work.

22. 1 Corinthians 11:30 - "That is why many among you are weak and sick, and a number of you have fallen asleep."

This verse about sickness indicates that some illness in the Corinthian church resulted from irreverent participation in communion. While sickness doesn't always result from sin, sometimes it does, and spiritual renewal is the remedy.

23. Proverbs 14:30 - "A heart at peace gives life to the body, but envy rots the bones."

This verse about sickness connects emotional and spiritual states to physical health. Peace promotes well-being; envy damages it. Mental and spiritual health significantly influence physical health.

Verses About Ultimate Healing and Hope

24. Revelation 21:4 - "He will wipe every tear from their eyes. There will be no more death or mourning or crying or pain, for the old order of things has passed away."

This final verse about sickness offers ultimate hope. While earthly sickness remains a reality, God's final work eliminates suffering forever. This verse should comfort believers facing persistent illness—ultimate healing and restoration are guaranteed in eternity.

25. 1 Corinthians 15:54-57 - "Death has been swallowed up in victory. 'Where, O death, is your victory? Where, O death, is your sting?' But thanks be to God, who gives us the victory through our Lord Jesus Christ."

This verse about sickness celebrates Christ's victory over death and disease through His resurrection. While sickness remains a present reality, Christ's resurrection guarantees ultimate victory over all suffering and death.

FAQ

Q: Which of these verses about sickness guarantees physical healing? A: No single verse guarantees it. While many verses describe healing, others acknowledge ongoing suffering. God's answer to prayer for healing is sometimes yes, sometimes no, and sometimes wait. All verses point to God's goodness regardless of physical outcomes.

Q: How should these verses about sickness change my perspective on illness? A: They should deepen your trust in God's compassion, encourage you to seek community prayer, remind you that sickness doesn't indicate spiritual failure, and anchor your hope in ultimate restoration through Christ.

Q: Can I use these verses about sickness to claim healing? A: Prayer and faith are biblical responses to sickness, but claiming specific outcomes can lead to disappointment. Instead, use these verses to draw near to God, trusting His wisdom about what's best for your spiritual journey.

Q: Do all these verses about sickness apply equally to modern believers? A: Yes, their core spiritual truths apply today. While healing miracles occurred in Scripture, believers today should also seek prayer, medical care, and community support in balanced approaches to sickness.

Q: Which verses about sickness should I memorize for encouragement? A: Consider memorizing James 5:13, 2 Corinthians 1:3-4, and Psalm 23:4. These provide encouragement to seek prayer, remember God's comfort, and trust His presence through illness.

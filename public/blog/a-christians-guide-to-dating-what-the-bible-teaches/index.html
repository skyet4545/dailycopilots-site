A Christian's Guide to Dating: What the Bible Teaches

By Bible Copilot Updated July 3, 2026 Scripture-cited · editorial standards
Quick answer

Your complete Christian's guide to dating with biblical wisdom. Learn Scripture-based principles for healthy relationships, boundaries, and faith-centered choices.

Introduction: A Christian's Guide to Dating

A Christian's guide to dating isn't about finding a perfect partner or following rigid rules. Rather, a Christian's guide to dating is about applying Scripture's timeless wisdom to modern relationship challenges. Every Christian navigating dating deserves a framework grounded in faith, and a Christian's guide to dating provides exactly that.

A Christian's guide to dating acknowledges that relationships are important to God and that how you date matters spiritually. Your romantic choices shape your character, influence your spiritual trajectory, and often lead to one of life's most significant commitments—marriage. A Christian's guide to dating equips you to make decisions you won't regret.

A Christian's Guide to Dating: Start With Your Foundation

Your Relationship With God Comes First

Matthew 6:33 states: "But seek first his kingdom and his righteousness, and all these things will be given to you as well."

A Christian's guide to dating begins by establishing that God comes before dating. This isn't legalistic or restrictive—it's foundational. When your primary relationship is with God, dating decisions become clearer:

  • You know your identity isn't dependent on having a romantic partner
  • You can assess potential partners objectively rather than desperately
  • You have standards you won't compromise
  • You trust God's timing even when loneliness tempts you

A Christian's guide to dating emphasizes that desperation makes poor choices. Peace with your singleness—whether temporary or permanent—creates the emotional freedom to date wisely.

Know Your Values Before You Date

A Christian's guide to dating suggests clarity before you start dating: - What are your core values? - What do you need in a partner beyond attraction? - What are your absolute non-negotiables? - What would you compromise on? - Where is your faith non-negotiable?

A Christian's guide to dating recognizes that knowing these things in advance prevents being swept away by chemistry.

A Christian's Guide to Dating: Choose Your Partner Wisely

The Non-Negotiable: Faith Alignment

2 Corinthians 6:14 provides A Christian's guide to dating's most critical principle: "Do not be unequally yoked with unbelievers. For what partnership has righteousness with lawlessness? Or what fellowship has light with darkness?"

A Christian's guide to dating makes clear that faith compatibility isn't optional. Why?

Because your faith shapes: - Your moral framework and daily decisions - How you handle conflict, forgiveness, and grace - Your financial priorities and approach to generosity - How you plan to raise children - Your understanding of purpose and meaning

A Christian's guide to dating emphasizes that you cannot build a shared life on fundamentally different spiritual foundations. If your partner doesn't share your faith, your deepest values will inevitably create tension.

Character Matters More Than Chemistry

Proverbs 31:10 reminds us: "A wife of noble character who can find? She is worth far more than rubies."

A Christian's guide to dating evaluates potential partners by their character: - Do they treat others with respect and kindness? - Are they honest and trustworthy? - Do they take responsibility for their mistakes? - Are they growing and striving to be better? - How do they treat those they disagree with?

A Christian's guide to dating recognizes that chemistry fades, but character endures. The person who's fun and attractive but dishonest or disrespectful won't become trustworthy through love.

A Christian's Guide to Dating: Protect Your Heart

Guard Your Emotional Well-Being

Proverbs 4:23 advises: "Above all else, guard your heart, for everything you do flows from it."

A Christian's guide to dating means: - Not sharing deep vulnerabilities with someone uncommitted to you - Noticing when you're becoming too attached too quickly - Recognizing when you're changing yourself to keep someone - Setting boundaries around how much time and energy you invest

A Christian's guide to dating doesn't mean being cold or closed off. It means being strategic with your emotional investment.

Pay Attention to Red Flags

Proverbs 22:3 states: "The prudent see danger and take refuge, but the simple keep going and pay the penalty."

A Christian's guide to dating teaches you to notice and respond to warning signs: - Does your dating partner respect your boundaries? - Do they pressure you to compromise your values? - Are they honest with you? - How do they handle your saying no? - Do they criticize your faith or try to pull you away from God?

A Christian's guide to dating emphasizes that love doesn't mean tolerating disrespect or compromise. Early intervention prevents deeper hurt.

A Christian's Guide to Dating: Maintain Purity

Sexual Boundaries Before Marriage

1 Corinthians 6:18-20 states: "Flee from sexual immorality. All other sins a person commits are outside the body, but whoever sins sexually, sins against their own body... Therefore honor God with your bodies."

A Christian's guide to dating makes clear that sexual boundaries matter. This isn't about shame—it's about honoring God and protecting the sacredness of sexual intimacy.

A Christian's guide to dating suggests: - Decide your physical boundaries before you need them - Communicate these boundaries to your dating partner clearly - Avoid situations that tempt compromise - Spend dating time in community, not only in private - Be honest about struggle if you're tempted to go further than you planned

Emotional Intimacy Also Matters

A Christian's guide to dating recognizes that emotional intimacy can be more powerful than physical. Don't share your deepest vulnerabilities with someone you're not committed to long-term.

A Christian's Guide to Dating: Move With Intention

Know Why You're Dating

A Christian's guide to dating means being intentional about relationships. Ask yourself: - Am I dating this person to avoid loneliness, or because I'm genuinely interested in their future? - Does this relationship align with my values and life goals? - Do I see potential for this to move toward commitment?

A Christian's guide to dating recognizes that casual dating isn't wrong, but indefinite casual dating without movement toward clarity wastes time and emotional energy.

Progress Relationships Thoughtfully

A Christian's guide to dating suggests moving relationships forward intentionally: - Early dating: Get to know their character and values - Growing connection: Deepen emotional intimacy and assess compatibility - Serious dating: Discuss future goals and consider marriage - Commitment: Make decisions about whether this is your person

A Christian's guide to dating doesn't mandate a specific timeline, but it discourages remaining in ambiguity indefinitely.

A Christian's Guide to Dating: Seek Community Wisdom

Don't Navigate Dating Alone

Proverbs 15:22 states: "Plans fail for lack of counsel, but with many advisers they succeed."

A Christian's guide to dating emphasizes community involvement: - Share about your dating relationship with trusted mentors - Ask close friends what they observe about your dating partner - Seek pastoral counsel about major relationship decisions - Listen when multiple people express concerns

A Christian's guide to dating recognizes that others often see patterns you're too emotionally involved to notice.

A Christian's Guide to Dating: Love as Action

Evaluate Love Through 1 Corinthians 13

"Love is patient, love is kind. It does not envy, it does not boast, it is not proud. It does not dishonor others, it is not self-seeking, it is not easily angered, it keeps no record of wrongs."

A Christian's guide to dating assesses whether your dating relationship reflects these qualities: - Does your partner treat you patiently? - Are they genuinely kind? - Do they respect your worth? - Are they generous, or self-focused? - How do they handle conflict?

A Christian's guide to dating teaches that real love is demonstrated through consistent, caring action—not just words or feelings.

A Christian's Guide to Dating: Trust God's Timing

When You're Waiting for the Right Person

Psalm 27:14 encourages: "Wait for the Lord; be strong and take heart and wait for the Lord."

A Christian's guide to dating for single people emphasizes: - Singleness is a legitimate, blessed state - Waiting well means investing in your growth, faith, and community - God's timing is better than your rushed timeline - Desperation often leads to poor choices

A Christian's guide to dating includes trusting that if marriage is part of God's plan, He will provide the right person.

A Christian's Guide to Dating: Healing After Heartbreak

When Relationships End

Psalm 147:3 reminds us: "He heals the brokenhearted and binds up their wounds."

A Christian's guide to dating acknowledges that heartbreak is often part of the dating journey. When relationships end, healing involves: - Grieving openly and honestly - Seeking community support - Returning to your relationship with God - Learning from what happened - Giving yourself time before dating again

FAQ

Q: A Christian's guide to dating—what if I've already failed to follow these principles? A: 1 John 1:9 promises forgiveness for those who confess their sins. Learn from the past and move forward with renewed commitment.

Q: A Christian's guide to dating—is any of this outdated? A: The principles address human hearts and relationships, which remain constant. They're as relevant today as when Scripture was written.

Q: A Christian's guide to dating—should I use dating apps? A: The biblical principles apply regardless of how you meet. Seek someone with shared faith, assess character, maintain boundaries, and involve community wisdom.

Q: A Christian's guide to dating—how long should I date before proposing? A: A Christian's guide to dating doesn't mandate a specific timeline, but most experts suggest 1-2 years allows sufficient time to assess compatibility through various seasons.

Q: A Christian's guide to dating—what if my family disagrees with my choice? A: Listen respectfully to their concerns, especially if multiple trusted people express worry. Your family may see what you're too emotionally involved to notice.

Top 25 Bible Verses About Forgiveness (With Explanations)

By Bible Copilot Updated July 3, 2026 Scripture-cited · editorial standards
Quick answer

Discover the top 25 Bible verses about forgiveness with detailed explanations to deepen your understanding of Scripture's healing message.

Whether you're struggling to forgive someone who's hurt you or seeking to understand God's perspective on forgiveness, Bible verses about forgiveness offer transformative wisdom. Scripture contains numerous verses about forgiveness that speak directly to the human heart and point us toward healing. These Bible verses about forgiveness range from direct commands to profound illustrations of grace, each offering unique insight into why forgiveness matters and how to practice it. This guide explores the most impactful Bible verses about forgiveness that can anchor your faith and transform your relationships.

Old Testament Bible Verses About Forgiveness

The Old Testament establishes important foundations for understanding forgiveness, showing how God's character has always reflected mercy and restoration.

Genesis 50:15-21 tells the story of Joseph forgiving his brothers: "When Joseph's brothers saw that their father was dead, they said, 'What if Joseph holds a grudge against us and pays us back for all the wrongs we did to him?' So they sent word to Joseph, saying... Joseph said to them, 'Don't be afraid. Am I in the place of God?... You intended to harm me, but God intended it for good.'" This narrative illustrates Bible verses about forgiveness through Joseph's refusal to take revenge and his ability to see God's purpose in his suffering.

Psalm 103:8-10 celebrates God's forgiving nature: "The Lord is compassionate and gracious, slow to anger, abounding in love. He will not always accuse, nor will he harbor his anger forever; he does not treat us as our sins deserve or repay us according to our iniquities." These Bible verses about forgiveness reveal God's character as fundamentally merciful. The psalmist emphasizes that God doesn't treat us according to what we deserve—He treats us with grace.

Proverbs 10:12 offers practical wisdom: "Hatred stirs up conflict, but love covers over all wrongs." Among Bible verses about forgiveness, this one highlights the power of love to overcome offense. "Covering" wrong doesn't mean ignoring it, but choosing not to publicize it or nurse it.

Isaiah 43:25 shows God's perspective: "I, even I, am he who blots out your transgressions, for my own sake, and remembers your sins no more." This stands among the most powerful Bible verses about forgiveness because it shows God actively choosing not to remember our sins—a radical picture of complete forgiveness.

Micah 7:18 asks: "Who is a God like you, who pardons sin and forgives the transgression of the remnant of his inheritance? You do not stay angry forever but delight to show mercy." These Bible verses about forgiveness celebrate God's character and suggest that showing mercy delights His heart.

New Testament Bible Verses About Forgiveness - From Jesus

Jesus taught extensively about forgiveness, making these Bible verses about forgiveness central to Christian practice.

Matthew 6:14-15 connects our forgiveness to God's: "For if you forgive other people when they sin against you, your heavenly Father will also forgive you. But if you do not forgive others their sins, your Father will not forgive your sins." Among all Bible verses about forgiveness, these words carry particular weight coming directly from Jesus, establishing forgiveness as essential to our relationship with God.

Matthew 18:21-22 addresses forgiveness without limits: "Then Peter came to Jesus and asked, 'Lord, how many times shall I forgive my brother or sister who sins against me? Up to seven times?' Jesus answered, 'I tell you, not seven times, but seventy-seven times.'" These Bible verses about forgiveness use an intentionally large number to convey that forgiveness shouldn't be rationed or counted.

Luke 6:37 states: "Do not judge, and you will not be judged. Do not condemn, and you will not be condemned. Forgive, and you will be forgiven." This collection of Bible verses about forgiveness ties together judgment, condemnation, and forgiveness as related spiritual practices.

Mark 11:25-26 combines forgiveness with prayer: "And when you stand praying, if you hold anything against anyone, forgive them, so that your Father in heaven may forgive your sins." These Bible verses about forgiveness show that holding grudges creates a barrier in our prayer life.

Luke 23:34 captures Jesus' own practice of forgiveness on the cross: "Jesus said, 'Father, forgive them, for they do not know what they are doing.'" Even while being crucified, Jesus prayed for His killers' forgiveness. These Bible verses about forgiveness demonstrate the ultimate example of grace in the face of injustice.

John 8:10-11 shows Jesus forgiving the woman caught in adultery: "Has no one condemned you?... Then neither do I condemn you... Go now and leave your life of sin." These Bible verses about forgiveness emphasize both restoration and the call away from destructive behavior.

Bible Verses About Forgiveness From Paul

Paul's letters contain extensive teaching on forgiveness, providing Bible verses about forgiveness that guide Christian practice.

Ephesians 4:31-32 instructs: "Get rid of all bitterness, rage and anger, brawling and slander, and every form of malice. Be kind and compassionate to one another, forgiving each other, just as in Christ God forgave you." These Bible verses about forgiveness give practical steps: remove negativity, replace it with kindness, and forgive as Christ forgave.

Colossians 3:12-14 encourages: "Therefore, as God's chosen people, holy and dearly loved, clothe yourselves with compassion, kindness, humility, gentleness and patience. Bear with each other and forgive whatever grievances you may have against one another. Forgive as the Lord forgave you. And over all these virtues put on love, which binds them all together in perfect unity." These Bible verses about forgiveness present it within a larger context of Christian character development.

2 Corinthians 5:17-19 describes reconciliation: "If anyone is in Christ, the new creation has come... All this is from God, who reconciled us to himself through Christ and gave us the ministry of reconciliation." These Bible verses about forgiveness show that forgiveness flows from our experience of God's reconciliation toward us.

Philippians 3:13-14 addresses moving forward: "Brothers and sisters, I do not consider myself yet to have taken hold of it. But one thing I do: Forgetting what is behind and straining toward what is ahead, I press on toward the goal." These Bible verses about forgiveness encourage us to not remain stuck in past offenses.

1 Timothy 1:15-16 shows God's mercy: "Here is a trustworthy saying that deserves full acceptance: Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners—of whom I am the worst. But for that very reason I was shown mercy so that in me... Christ Jesus might display his immense patience as an example." These Bible verses about forgiveness illustrate that experiencing God's forgiveness enables us to extend it to others.

Bible Verses About Forgiveness on Overcoming Unforgiveness

Some Bible verses about forgiveness directly address the damage that unforgiveness causes.

Hebrews 12:15 warns: "See to it that no one falls short of the grace of God and that no bitter root grows up to cause trouble and defile many." These Bible verses about forgiveness show that unforgiveness creates spiritual damage beyond the unforgiven person.

Proverbs 17:9 suggests: "Whoever would foster love covers over an offense, but whoever repeats the matter separates close friends." These Bible verses about forgiveness indicate that constantly rehearsing wrongs prevents healing.

Matthew 12:34-35 teaches: "For the mouth speaks what the heart is full of. A good man brings good things out of the good stored up in him, and an evil man brings evil things out of the evil stored up in him." These Bible verses about forgiveness show that our speech reflects our heart condition regarding forgiveness.

1 Peter 3:9 directs: "Do not repay evil with evil or insult with insult. On the contrary, repay evil with blessing, because to this you were called so that you may inherit a blessing." These Bible verses about forgiveness teach the unexpected principle of blessing those who hurt us.

Proverbs 19:11 notes: "A person's wisdom yields patience; it is to one's glory to overlook an offense." These Bible verses about forgiveness present overlooking offense as a mark of maturity and wisdom.

Bible Verses About Forgiveness on God's Work

Finally, some Bible verses about forgiveness emphasize God's role in enabling our forgiveness.

Psalm 130:3-4 acknowledges: "If you, Lord, kept a record of sins, Lord, who could stand? But with you there is forgiveness, so that we can, with reverence, serve you." These Bible verses about forgiveness show that God's willingness to not keep records makes our faith possible.

1 John 1:9 promises: "If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just and will forgive us our sins and purify us from all unrighteousness." These Bible verses about forgiveness assure us of God's character—He is both just and merciful in extending forgiveness.

FAQ

Q: What Bible verses about forgiveness are most important to memorize? A: Matthew 6:14-15, Ephesians 4:31-32, and Colossians 3:13 capture the essence of forgiveness as taught throughout Scripture.

Q: Do these Bible verses about forgiveness require forgiving abusers? A: These verses call for forgiveness but not necessarily for ongoing relationship with abusive individuals. Forgiveness and protective boundaries are compatible.

Q: How should I use Bible verses about forgiveness in my daily life? A: Meditate on verses when conflict arises, pray them back to God, and ask His Spirit to help you apply their truth.

Q: Are there Bible verses about forgiveness for when I can't forgive? A: Yes. Verses about God's patience (Proverbs 19:11) and Paul's discussion of pressing forward (Philippians 3:13-14) acknowledge the difficulty.

Q: What Bible verses about forgiveness address self-forgiveness? A: While not explicitly about self-forgiveness, verses about God's complete forgiveness (Psalm 103:10, Isaiah 43:25) help us extend that same grace to ourselves.

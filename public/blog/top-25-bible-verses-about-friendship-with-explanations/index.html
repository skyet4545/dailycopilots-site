Top 25 Bible Verses About Friendship (With Explanations)

By Bible Copilot Updated July 3, 2026 Scripture-cited · editorial standards
Quick answer

Explore the top 25 Bible verses about friendship with detailed explanations. Discover Scripture's wisdom on building meaningful Christian friendships.

Introduction

For those seeking biblical guidance on friendship, a carefully curated list of top 25 Bible verses about friendship provides comprehensive wisdom across Old Testament principles, New Testament teaching, and practical application. These top 25 Bible verses about friendship cover multiple dimensions: the nature of authentic friendship, the importance of choosing friends wisely, the transformative power of friendship, the role of honesty and accountability, and the healing power of forgiveness. Whether you're establishing new friendships, deepening existing connections, or navigating relational challenges, the top 25 Bible verses about friendship offer guidance grounded in God's Word.

This collection of the top 25 Bible verses about friendship isn't randomly selected. Instead, these verses represent the Bible's most significant teachings about human connection, providing a foundation for understanding what Scripture really teaches about friendship. Whether used for personal reflection, Bible study, or teaching, these top 25 Bible verses about friendship illuminate God's perspective on relationships and offer practical wisdom for contemporary Christian life.

The Top 25 Bible Verses About Friendship

1. Proverbs 17:17 - "A friend loves at all times, and a brother is born for adversity." This verse captures the essence of biblical friendship. Authentic friendship isn't conditional or situational—it's marked by consistent, faithful love that particularly shines during hardship. This teaching emphasizes that true friends don't disappear when circumstances become difficult.

2. Proverbs 13:20 - "Walk with the wise and become wise, for a companion of fools suffers harm." This verse reveals friendship's transformative power. Our friends shape our character and spiritual direction. Wise friend selection becomes essential, as spending time with certain people inevitably influences who we become.

3. Proverbs 27:17 - "As iron sharpens iron, so one person sharpens another." Through this powerful metaphor, Scripture teaches that friendships refine us. Just as a whetstone sharpens a blade, genuine friends help us develop virtue, deepen wisdom, and overcome weakness through their influence.

4. John 15:14-15 - "You are my friends if you do what I command. I no longer call you servants, because a servant does not know his master's business. Instead, I have called you friends, for everything that I have learned from my Father I have made known to you." Jesus revolutionizes friendship by elevating His disciples to friends and offering them complete transparency. This verse teaches that authentic friendship includes vulnerability and sharing knowledge, purpose, and heart.

5. John 15:12-13 - "My command is this: Love each other as I have loved you. Greater love has no one than this: to lay down one's life for one's friends." Jesus establishes the standard of sacrificial love for friendship. This verse teaches that biblical friendship isn't comfortable—it involves self-giving love modeled on Christ's willingness to die for those He loves.

6. 1 Samuel 18:1-3 - "After David had finished talking with Saul, Jonathan became one in spirit with David, and he loved him as himself... And Jonathan made a covenant with David because he loved him as himself." This narrative establishes the model of covenantal friendship. David and Jonathan's bond demonstrates friendship as a sacred agreement made before God, marked by complete unity and mutual commitment.

7. 1 Samuel 20:42 - "Jonathan said to David, 'Go in peace, for we have sworn friendship with each other in the name of the Lord.'" This verse illustrates that genuine friendships are sealed in God's presence. The concept of friendship made "in the name of the Lord" emphasizes that authentic relationships are spiritual commitments.

8. Proverbs 27:5-6 - "Better is open rebuke than hidden love. Wounds from a friend can be trusted, but an enemy multiplies kisses." Scripture teaches that true friends tell difficult truths because they care more about your growth than your approval. Honest feedback, though sometimes painful, reflects genuine friendship far more than empty flattery.

9. Proverbs 27:10 - "Do not forsake your friend or a friend of your family, nor go to your relative's house when disaster strikes you." This verse emphasizes loyalty and consistency in friendship. True friends maintain connection during both prosperous and difficult seasons, showing particular devotion during adversity.

10. Proverbs 22:24-25 - "Do not make friends with an angry man, do not associate with one easily angered, or you may learn his ways and get yourself ensnared." Scripture warns against friendships that trap us in destructive patterns. This verse emphasizes wise friend selection, protecting ourselves from relationships that would ensnare us in harmful behaviors.

11. 2 Corinthians 6:14 - "Do not be yoked together with unbelievers. For what do they have in common? Or what fellowship can light have with darkness?" While addressing marriage, this principle applies to close friendships. Scripture suggests that your deepest relationships should be with those who share your faith commitment and encourage your spiritual growth.

12. Proverbs 13:1 - "A wise son heeds his father's instruction, but a mocker does not listen to rebuke." This verse teaches the importance of receiving correction from those who love you. Wise people accept feedback from friends as gifts that help them grow and improve.

13. 1 Thessalonians 5:11 - "Therefore encourage one another and build each other up, just as in fact you are doing." Paul teaches that encouragement is a spiritual practice essential to community. Friends strengthen one another through words and actions that build faith, courage, and spiritual maturity.

14. Hebrews 10:24-25 - "And let us consider how we may spur one another on toward love and good deeds, not giving up meeting together, as some are in the habit of doing, but encouraging one another." This verse emphasizes that regular gathering and mutual encouragement are essential to spiritual development. Christian friendship requires consistent presence and active engagement.

15. Ecclesiastes 4:9-10 - "Two are better than one, because they have a good return for their labor: If either of them falls down, one can help the other up. But pity anyone who falls and has no one to help them up." Scripture affirms that we need companions—people who help carry our burdens and support us through difficulty. Friendship serves practical necessity, not luxury, in human life.

16. Colossians 3:13 - "Bear with each other and forgive one another if any of you has a grievance against someone. Forgive as the Lord forgave you." This verse teaches that friendships survive conflict through forgiveness and grace. When imperfect people relate, misunderstanding is inevitable—yet forgiveness enables reconciliation.

17. Matthew 18:15-17 - "If your brother or sister sins, go and point out their fault, just between the two of you. If they listen to you, you have won them over." Jesus provides the pathway for resolving relational conflict. Direct, humble conversation about issues, undertaken privately and in love, can resolve most disagreements and restore friendship.

18. Proverbs 28:23 - "Whoever rebukes a person will in the end gain favor than one who has a flattering tongue." Scripture affirms that truthfulness ultimately strengthens relationships. While honest feedback may initially seem harsh, it builds trust and respect far more than empty flattery.

19. Psalm 55:12-14 - "If an enemy were insulting me, I could endure it... But it is you, a man like myself, my companion, my close friend, with whom I once enjoyed sweet fellowship." This verse acknowledges the particular pain of friendship betrayal. Scripture validates the deep hurt that comes when trusted friends disappoint or betray us.

20. Psalm 55:22 - "Cast your cares on him for he cares for you." When friends fail, God remains faithful. This verse directs us toward God's presence and care when human relationships disappoint us.

21. Proverbs 22:3 - "The prudent see danger and take refuge, but the simple keep going and pay the penalty." This verse supports protecting yourself from toxic relationships. Wise stewardship includes recognizing when friendships are unhealthy and creating appropriate distance.

22. James 5:16 - "Therefore confess your sins to each other and pray for each other so that you may be healed." Scripture teaches that vulnerability and honest disclosure deepen friendship and facilitate healing. Authentic friendship requires opening your true self to trusted companions.

23. Proverbs 27:12 - "Faithful are the wounds of a friend, but deceitful are the kisses of an enemy." This verse distinguishes true friends from false ones. Real friends care enough to offer correction; enemies offer only false comfort and agreement.

24. Ephesians 4:2-3 - "Be completely humble and gentle; be patient, bearing with one another in love. Make every effort to keep the unity of the Spirit through the bond of peace." Maintaining friendships requires intentional effort, humility, gentleness, and patience. Authentic relationships are preserved through commitment to unity and peace.

25. Proverbs 27:10 - "Do not forsake your friend...nor go to your relative's house when disaster strikes you—better a neighbor nearby than a relative far away." This verse emphasizes that physical proximity and presence matter in friendship. Show up for friends during difficult seasons; your presence demonstrates genuine care and loyalty.

FAQ

Q: Are these 25 verses exhaustive on friendship? A: These 25 verses represent the Bible's core teachings on friendship, covering principles, examples, and applications. Additional verses address related topics like community and encouragement, but these 25 capture friendship's essential biblical perspective.

Q: How can I study these verses more deeply? A: Use Bible Copilot's study modes to explore context, historical background, and applications. Consider journaling about which verses speak to your current relational season and how to apply them.

Q: Which verses apply if I'm struggling with friendship conflict? A: Matthew 18:15-17 provides process for resolution. Colossians 3:13 teaches forgiveness, and Ephesians 4:2-3 emphasizes the effort required to maintain relationships through difficulty.

Q: Which verses help me choose friends wisely? A: Proverbs 13:20, 22:24-25, 2 Corinthians 6:14, and Proverbs 13:1 provide guidance on selecting companions who will help you grow spiritually and avoid those who would harm your character.

Q: Which verses encourage me when feeling lonely? A: Ecclesiastes 4:9-10 validates your longing for connection. Hebrews 10:24-25 encourages gathering with Christian community. These verses affirm that friendship is essential to flourishing.

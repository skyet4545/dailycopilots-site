Top 25 Bible Verses About Tithing (With Explanations)

By Bible Copilot Updated July 3, 2026 Scripture-cited · editorial standards
Quick answer

Discover the top 25 Bible verses about tithing with clear explanations. Explore Scripture's teaching on tithes, their purpose, and spiritual meaning.

Introduction

The Bible addresses tithing throughout both testaments. These top 25 Bible verses about tithing range from Old Testament commands to New Testament principles, addressing tithing from multiple theological angles. Whether you're studying the practice, seeking to understand its biblical basis, or exploring what Scripture says about tithing's spiritual significance, these top Bible verses about tithing provide comprehensive coverage. Let's explore what these passages teach about this important practice.

Foundation and Purpose

1. Leviticus 27:30 - "A tithe of everything from the land, whether grain from the soil or fruit from the trees, belongs to the Lord; it is holy to the Lord." This establishes the most basic biblical principle about tithing. God claims a tithe as holy. This wasn't an optional offering but a fundamental requirement.

2. Numbers 18:21 - "I give to the Levites all the tithes in Israel as their inheritance in return for the work they do while serving at the tent of meeting." This explains tithing's primary purpose—supporting those who served full-time in religious leadership. The Levites had no land inheritance, so tithes became their provision.

3. Deuteronomy 14:28-29 - "At the end of every three years, bring all the tithes of that year's produce and store it in your towns, so that the Levites... and the foreigner, the fatherless and the widow... shall come and eat and be satisfied." This reveals tithing's justice dimension. Every third year, tithes specifically targeted vulnerable populations, showing that tithing was always about community care.

4. Deuteronomy 14:22-23 - "Be sure to set aside a tenth of all that your fields produce each year... so that you may learn to revere the Lord your God always." Tithing's purpose included spiritual formation. The practice itself was meant to teach believers reverence for God and trust in His provision.

5. Deuteronomy 26:12 - "When you have finished setting aside a tenth of all your produce in the third year, the year of the tithe, you shall give it to the Levite, the foreigner, the fatherless and the widow." This reinforces tithing's role in supporting those unable to support themselves, showing God's consistent concern for vulnerable populations.

Promises About Return

6. Malachi 3:10 - "Bring the whole tithe into the storehouse... and see if I will not throw open the floodgates of heaven and pour out so much blessing that there will not be room enough to store it." This presents one of Scripture's most dramatic promises about tithing. Faithful giving opens channels of divine blessing that exceed what we might expect.

7. Proverbs 3:9-10 - "Honor the Lord with your wealth, with the firstfruits of all your crops; then your barns will be filled to overflowing, and your vats will brim with new wine." This promises that honoring God with tithes produces abundance. The principle of return flows from faithful giving.

8. Proverbs 11:24-25 - "One person gives freely, yet gains even more; another withholds unduly, but comes to poverty. A generous person will prosper; whoever refreshes others will be refreshed." This paradox teaches that generosity, not hoarding, produces increase. Tithing demonstrates this principle in practice.

9. Luke 6:38 - "Give, and it will be given to you. A good measure, pressed down, shaken together and running over, will be poured into your lap." Jesus teaches that generous giving produces generous return. This isn't transactional but reflects God's generous character.

Jesus's Perspective on Tithing

10. Matthew 23:23 - "Woe to you, teachers of the law and Pharisees, you hypocrites! You give a tenth of your spices—mint, dill and cumin. But you have neglected the more important matters of the law—justice, mercy and faithfulness." Jesus critiques those who tithe but neglect justice and mercy. Tithing alone, without righteousness, misses the deeper point.

11. Mark 12:41-44 - "Jesus sat down opposite the place where the offerings were put and watched the crowd putting their money into the temple treasury... But a poor widow... put in two very small copper coins... Jesus said, 'I tell you the truth, this poor widow has put more into the treasury than all the others.'" The widow's two coins exemplify what tithing truly means—sacrificial giving from faith. Jesus evaluates generosity by sacrifice, not amount.

12. Luke 12:33 - "Sell your possessions and give to the poor. Provide purses for yourselves that will not wear out, a treasure in heaven that will never fail, where no thief comes near and no moth destroys." Jesus invites generosity potentially exceeding standard tithes. He prioritizes eternal treasure over earthly accumulation.

13. Matthew 6:19-21 - "Do not store up for yourselves treasures on earth... But store up for yourselves treasures in heaven... For where your treasure is, there your heart will be also." Jesus teaches that tithing should express our true values—heavenly treasure over earthly wealth.

Heart Motivation

14. 2 Corinthians 9:7 - "Each of you should give what you have decided in your heart to give, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver." Paul emphasizes that the attitude behind tithing matters supremely. God values joyful, willing generosity over obligation.

15. 1 Corinthians 13:3 - "If I give all I possess to the poor and give over my body to hardship that I might boast, but do not have love, I gain nothing." Paul teaches that tithing without love is spiritually empty. The heart must motivate all giving.

16. Proverbs 23:7 - "As a man thinks in his heart, so is he." Our thinking about possessions shapes our tithing. Tithing requires transformed thinking about ownership and stewardship.

17. Matthew 6:1-4 - "Be careful not to do your 'acts of righteousness' before others... But when you give to the needy, do not let your left hand know what your right hand is doing, so that your giving may be in secret." Jesus teaches that tithing should be private, free from the need for recognition or credit.

Trust and Provision

18. Matthew 6:25-26 - "Therefore I tell you, do not worry about your life, what you will eat or drink... Look at the birds of the air; they do not sow or reap... and yet your heavenly Father feeds them. Are you not much more valuable than they?" Jesus grounds tithing in trust. Those who tithe can trust that God will provide for their needs.

19. Philippians 4:19 - "And my God will meet all your needs according to the riches of his glory in Christ Jesus." Paul promises that God will provide for faithful givers. Tithing demonstrates trust that God will sustain us.

20. Proverbs 22:9 - "The generous will themselves be blessed, for they share their food with the poor." Generosity produces blessing. Those who tithe and give to the poor experience God's blessing.

Modern Application

21. 1 Corinthians 16:1-2 - "On the first day of every week, each one of you should set aside a sum of money in keeping with your income, saving it up." Paul recommends regular, proportional tithing. This models how tithing should be practiced—consistently and according to capacity.

22. 2 Corinthians 8:12 - "For if the willingness is there, the gift is acceptable according to what one has, not according to what one does not have." This reassures those with limited means. Proportional tithing from those struggling is more valuable than large gifts from those with abundance.

23. 1 Peter 4:10 - "Each of you should use whatever gift you have received to serve others, as faithful stewards of God's grace in its various forms." Tithing is one aspect of stewardship. We're accountable for how we use all God's gifts—time, talents, and treasure.

24. Psalm 24:1 - "The earth is the Lord's, and everything in it, the world, and all who live in it." This reminds us that everything belongs to God. Tithing reflects this truth and our role as stewards.

25. Romans 14:23 - "But whoever has doubts is condemned if they eat, because their eating is not from faith; and everything that does not come from faith is sin." This principle applies to tithing—it should flow from faith and conviction, not doubt or compulsion.

FAQ

Q: Why are there so many verses about tithing in the Bible? A: Tithing was a foundational practice in Israel, central to community life and God's economy. Its importance is reflected throughout Scripture.

Q: Do all these verses mean tithing is required for Christians today? A: These verses show tithing's historical importance and principles. The New Testament doesn't mandate specific percentages, but the principle—proportional, faithful giving—remains important.

Q: How do I choose which verses about tithing apply to my life? A: Consider both Old Testament commands and New Testament principles. Many Christians find the ten percent framework helpful, while others give differently based on conviction and capacity.

Q: What's the most important verse about tithing? A: 2 Corinthians 9:7 captures the essence—"God loves a cheerful giver." The attitude behind tithing matters supremely.

Q: How do these verses connect to modern Christian practice? A: The principles—proportional giving, trust in God's provision, supporting God's work, caring for vulnerable populations—remain foundational to Christian stewardship today.

Top 25 Bible Verses About Pride (With Explanations)

By Bible Copilot Updated July 3, 2026 Scripture-cited · editorial standards
Quick answer

Discover 25 powerful Bible verses about pride with explanations. Learn what Scripture teaches about spiritual pride and humility.

Pride is one of the most dangerous spiritual obstacles a believer can face. The Bible consistently warns about the destructive nature of pride, showing how it separates us from God and damages our relationships. Whether you're struggling with arrogance, self-importance, or spiritual superiority, Scripture offers clear guidance. This comprehensive guide explores 25 Bible verses about pride with detailed explanations, helping you understand what the Bible truly teaches about this critical character issue and how to overcome it.

Understanding Pride in Scripture

The Bible addresses pride in profound ways throughout both the Old and New Testaments. Pride—defined as excessive self-esteem or self-importance—stands directly opposed to the humility God desires in His followers. When we examine Bible verses about pride, we discover that Scripture doesn't merely suggest avoiding pride; it explicitly warns that pride precedes destruction.

Proverbs 16:18 declares, "Pride goes before destruction, a haughty spirit before a fall." This verse encapsulates the central biblical truth about pride. It's not simply about feeling good about yourself; it's about the spiritual and practical consequences that follow pride. The Bible verses about pride reveal a consistent message: pride blocks our relationship with God, prevents spiritual growth, and leads to personal ruin.

The 25 Most Important Bible Verses About Pride

1. Proverbs 16:18

"Pride goes before destruction, a haughty spirit before a fall."

This foundational verse about pride shows the inevitable consequences of arrogance. The Bible verses about pride repeatedly demonstrate that God opposes those who are proud.

2. Proverbs 11:2

"When pride comes, then comes disgrace, but with humility comes wisdom."

Here we see the direct contrast—pride brings shame while humility brings God's wisdom. This verse about pride illustrates the spiritual trade-off.

3. Proverbs 13:10

"Where there is strife, there is pride, but those who take advice are wise."

This verse about pride connects arrogance to conflict. Bible verses about pride show that proud people create division because they won't listen to others.

4. 1 Peter 5:5

"Young men, in the same way be submissive to those who are older. All of you, clothe yourselves with humility toward one another, because, 'God opposes the proud but shows favor to the humble.'"

This powerful verse about pride emphasizes God's active opposition toward prideful people, while showing favor to the humble.

5. Proverbs 8:13

"To fear the Lord is to hate evil; I hate pride and arrogance, evil behavior and perverse speech."

This verse about pride ranks it among the evils God despises. The Bible verses about pride reveal that arrogance is fundamentally offensive to God.

6. Proverbs 29:23

"A man's pride brings him low, but a man of lowly spirit gains honor."

Again, Bible verses about pride show the reversal principle—those who exalt themselves will be humbled, and the humble will be exalted.

7. Proverbs 15:25

"The Lord tears down the house of the proud, but he sets the widow's boundary stones firm."

This verse about pride demonstrates God's practical opposition to pride through His divine judgment.

8. Isaiah 13:11

"I will punish the world for its evil, the wicked for their sins. I will put an end to the arrogance of the haughty and will humble the pride of the ruthless."

Bible verses about pride in Isaiah show God's judgment specifically targeting proud and arrogant people.

9. Luke 18:14

"I tell you that this man, rather than the other, went home justified before God. For all those who exalt themselves will be humbled, and those who humble themselves will be exalted."

This verse about pride in Jesus's parable shows the spiritual principle clearly: pride leads to humbling, humility leads to exaltation.

10. Proverbs 21:4

"Haughty eyes and a proud heart—the unplowed field of the wicked—produce sin."

Bible verses about pride reveal that arrogance is the source from which sin springs.

11. Colossians 2:18

"Do not let anyone who delights in false humility and the worship of angels disqualify you for the prize. Such a person also goes into great detail about what they have seen, and their unspiritual mind puffs them up with idle notions."

This verse about pride warns against spiritual pride disguised as humility—a subtle form of arrogance.

12. James 4:6

"But he gives us more grace. That is why Scripture says: 'God opposes the proud but shows favor to the humble.'"

Bible verses about pride repeatedly emphasize God's opposition to the proud.

13. Proverbs 27:2

"Let someone else praise you, and not your own mouth; an outsider, and not your own lips."

This verse about pride teaches against self-promotion and self-praise—forms of arrogance.

14. Deuteronomy 8:14

"Then your heart will become proud and you will forget the Lord your God, who brought you out of Egypt, out of the land of slavery."

Bible verses about pride show how arrogance causes us to forget God and take credit for His blessings.

15. 2 Chronicles 26:16

"But after Uzziah became powerful, his pride led to his downfall. He was unfaithful to the Lord his God, and entered the temple of the Lord to burn incense on the altar."

This verse about pride provides a historical example of how arrogance led to spiritual rebellion and judgment.

16. Proverbs 18:12

"Before his downfall a man's heart is proud, but humility comes before honor."

Bible verses about pride consistently show this pattern: pride precedes loss, humility precedes gain.

17. Proverbs 30:32

"If you have played the fool and exalted yourself, or if you have planned evil, clap your hand over your mouth."

This verse about pride suggests that the response to realizing our arrogance should be silence and restraint.

18. Romans 12:3

"For by the grace given me I say to every one of you: Do not think of yourself more highly than you ought, but rather think of yourself with sober judgment, in accordance with the faith God has distributed to each of you."

Bible verses about pride in Paul's teaching emphasize having an accurate, sober view of ourselves.

19. Proverbs 14:3

"A fool's mouth lashes out with pride, but the lips of the wise protect them."

This verse about pride shows that prideful speech reveals foolishness.

20. 1 John 2:16

"For everything in the world—the lust of the flesh, the lust of the eyes, and the pride of life—comes not from the Father but from the world."

Bible verses about pride identify arrogance as a worldly value opposed to God's kingdom.

21. Obadiah 1:3

"The pride of your heart has deceived you, you who live in the clefts of the rocks and make your home on the heights, you who say to yourself, 'Who can bring me down to the ground?'"

This verse about pride shows how arrogance blinds us and leads to delusion.

22. Ezekiel 28:2

"Son of man, say to the ruler of Tyre, 'This is what the Sovereign Lord says: In the pride of your heart you say, "I am a god."'"

Bible verses about pride reveal how extreme arrogance can lead to blasphemous claims.

23. Proverbs 3:34

"He mocks proud mockers but shows favor to the humble and oppressed."

This verse about pride indicates that God actively opposes those who are proud.

24. Habakkuk 2:4

"See, the enemy is puffed up; his desires are not upright—but the righteous person will live by their faithfulness."

Bible verses about pride contrast spiritual arrogance with faithful righteousness.

25. Proverbs 12:15

"The way of fools seems right to them, but the wise listen to advice."

This final verse about pride shows that proud people reject counsel because they think they're already right.

Why Pride Matters in Your Christian Walk

Understanding what Bible verses about pride teach is essential for spiritual maturity. Pride isn't simply an annoying character flaw—it's a spiritual condition that separates us from God's grace. Throughout Scripture, Bible verses about pride demonstrate that God actively opposes prideful people while extending favor to the humble.

When you study Bible verses about pride, you discover that arrogance affects every area of life: relationships suffer, spiritual growth stalls, and we become vulnerable to deception. The humble person, by contrast, remains open to God's correction, continues learning, and experiences His blessing.

Living in Humility: The Antidote to Pride

The good news is that Bible verses about pride also point to a solution: choosing humility. Humility isn't self-deprecation or low self-esteem; it's an accurate view of yourself in relation to God. It means recognizing that every good gift comes from Him and that our worth is found in being His beloved children, not in our accomplishments or status.

As you reflect on these 25 Bible verses about pride, ask yourself: Where might pride be affecting your relationships, your service, or your spiritual growth? The Bible verses about pride offer not judgment but an invitation—an opportunity to align yourself with God's design and experience the blessing that comes from humble dependence on Him.

FAQ

Q: What does the Bible say is the root cause of pride? A: The Bible identifies pride as stemming from forgetting God and taking personal credit for His blessings. Deuteronomy 8:14 warns that prosperity and power can lead to pride when we forget that God is the source of all we have.

Q: Is it sinful to be proud of your accomplishments? A: The Bible distinguishes between acknowledging your efforts and pridefully boasting. Proverbs 27:2 teaches that it's appropriate to let others praise you, suggesting that self-promotion is the issue, not healthy self-respect.

Q: How does pride damage spiritual growth? A: Pride closes us to God's correction and prevents us from seeking His wisdom. Proverbs 11:2 shows that pride brings shame, while humility brings wisdom. A proud heart won't receive needed guidance.

Q: Can pride exist among church leaders and Christians? A: Yes, Colossians 2:18 warns against spiritual pride—when people believe they're spiritually superior or have special knowledge. This subtle form of pride is particularly dangerous because it hides behind religious language.

Q: What's the first step toward overcoming pride? A: Recognizing pride's presence in your life is the first step. Romans 12:3 encourages thinking of yourself with "sober judgment." Ask the Holy Spirit to reveal areas where pride influences your thinking and behavior.

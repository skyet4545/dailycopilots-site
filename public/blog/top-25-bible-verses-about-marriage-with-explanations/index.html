Top 25 Bible Verses About Marriage (With Explanations)

By Bible Copilot Updated July 3, 2026 Scripture-cited · editorial standards
Quick answer

Explore the 25 most important Bible verses about marriage. Each verse includes explanation and practical application for your Christian marriage.

Introduction

Bible verses about marriage provide the scriptural foundation for understanding God's design for this sacred relationship. Throughout Scripture, specific verses about marriage address purpose, responsibility, intimacy, conflict, and lasting commitment. Studying Bible verses about marriage allows couples to ground their relationships in God's Word rather than cultural pressure or personal emotion. These verses about marriage span Genesis through Revelation, showing that God has been consistent in His teaching about this cornerstone relationship.

Bible verses about marriage teach practical wisdom for navigating one of life's most significant commitments. Whether you're contemplating marriage, celebrating its blessings, facing challenges, or seeking to strengthen your union, relevant Bible verses about marriage offer guidance, encouragement, and hope. This collection of carefully selected verses about marriage provides comprehensive coverage of Scripture's teaching on this vital topic.

Foundational Verses About Marriage: God's Design and Purpose

1. Genesis 2:24 - "That is why a man leaves his father and mother and is united to his wife, and they become one flesh."

This foundational verse about marriage appears in Jesus' teaching (Matthew 19:5) and Paul's letters (Ephesians 5:31), emphasizing its importance. Verses about marriage that focus on "one flesh" reveal marriage as complete union—physical, emotional, spiritual, and practical. This verse about marriage teaches that marital union transcends biological connection to create genuine interdependence.

2. Genesis 1:27-28 - "So God created mankind in his own image, in the image of God he created them; male and female he created them. God blessed them and said to them, 'Be fruitful and increase in number.'"

Early verses about marriage connect it to God's image-bearing purpose. This verse about marriage shows that creating families and bearing God's image involves the partnership of male and female. Verses about marriage that reference creation reveal God's intentional design.

3. Proverbs 18:22 - "He who finds a wife finds what is good and receives favor from the Lord."

This verse about marriage celebrates marriage as blessing. Among verses about marriage, this one emphasizes that finding the right partner represents God's favor. This verse about marriage suggests that marital companionship is genuinely good and reflects God's generosity.

4. Proverbs 31:10-12 - "A wife of noble character who can find? She is worth far more than rubies. Her husband has full confidence in her and lacks nothing of value. She brings him good, not harm, all the days of her life."

These verses about marriage celebrate wives' tremendous value. Verses about marriage found in Proverbs 31 show that women are essential to marriage's flourishing, contributing wisdom, strength, and goodness. These verses about marriage counteract cultural messages diminishing women's value.

Verses About Marriage and Love's Expression

5. Song of Songs 2:16 - "My beloved is mine and I am his."

Among verses about marriage exploring intimacy, these words express possession and belonging. This verse about marriage shows that lovers should actively claim one another and celebrate being claimed. Verses about marriage like this affirm the beauty of exclusivity and deep connection.

6. Song of Songs 4:9-11 - "You have stolen my heart, my sister, my bride; you have stolen my heart with one glance of your eyes... How delightful is your love, my sister, my bride!"

These verses about marriage celebrate physical attraction and erotic joy. Verses about marriage in Song of Songs affirm that desire is not shameful but beautiful within marriage. These verses about marriage show that Scripture values sensual pleasure between spouses.

7. 1 Corinthians 13:4-5 - "Love is patient, love is kind. It does not envy, it does not boast, it is not proud. It does not dishonor others, it is not self-seeking, it is not easily angered, it keeps no record of wrongs."

Though not specifically verses about marriage, 1 Corinthians 13 defines love that marriage requires. These verses about marriage's foundation remind couples that biblical love is active choice, not mere emotion. Verses about marriage emphasizing patient, kind, forgiving love provide a standard for marital interactions.

8. Ephesians 5:25 - "Husbands, love your wives, just as Christ loved the church and gave himself up for her."

Critical verses about marriage addressing husbands' roles, this verse about marriage demands sacrificial love. Among verses about marriage calling for radical commitment, this one sets the standard highest. Verses about marriage like this challenge men to love their wives more than comfort or ease.

Verses About Marriage Roles and Responsibilities

9. Ephesians 5:22-24 - "Wives, submit yourselves to your own husbands as you do to the Lord. For the husband is the head of the family, just as Christ is the head of the church, his body, of which he is the Savior. Now as the church submits to Christ, so also wives should submit to their husbands in everything."

Among verses about marriage addressing wives' roles, these require careful understanding. Verses about marriage that mention submission must be balanced with Ephesians 5:21 on mutual submission. These verses about marriage describe voluntary alignment with a husband's spiritual leadership within a framework of love and mutual respect.

10. Ephesians 5:21 - "Submit to one another out of reverence for Christ."

Essential verses about marriage, these words provide context for role passages. Verses about marriage emphasizing mutual submission prevent misuse of authority. This verse about marriage shows that both spouses yield their desires to serve one another and honor Christ.

11. Colossians 3:18-19 - "Wives, submit yourselves to your husbands, as is fitting in the Lord. Husbands, love your wives and do not be harsh with them."

Verses about marriage balancing submission with gentleness, this passage shows that both spouses have responsibilities. Verses about marriage require wives' submission and husbands' loving-kindness simultaneously. These verses about marriage prevent either partner from dominating or being dominated.

12. 1 Peter 3:7 - "Likewise, husbands, live with your wives in an understanding way, showing honor to the woman as the weaker vessel, since they are heirs with you of the grace of life, so that your prayers may not be hindered."

Among verses about marriage addressing husbands' obligations, this one emphasizes understanding and honor. These verses about marriage teach that prayer effectiveness depends on marital health. Verses about marriage like this show that spiritual life and marriage are deeply intertwined.

Verses About Marriage and Sexual Intimacy

13. 1 Corinthians 7:3-5 - "The husband should fulfill his marital duty to his wife, and likewise the wife to her husband. The wife does not have authority over her own body but yields it to her husband. In the same way, the husband does not have authority over his own body but yields it to his wife. Do not deprive each other except perhaps by mutual consent and for a time, so that you may devote yourselves to prayer."

Verses about marriage addressing physical intimacy, these treat sex as spiritually significant and non-negotiable. Verses about marriage teach that withholding sex violates the marriage covenant. These verses about marriage show that sexual relation reinforces emotional and spiritual bonds.

14. Hebrews 13:4 - "Marriage should be honored by all, and the marriage bed kept pure, for God will judge the adulterer and all the sexually immoral."

Among verses about marriage protecting marital fidelity, this verse about marriage demands exclusivity and purity. Verses about marriage like this show that God takes seriously violations of sexual covenant. These verses about marriage warn of judgment against infidelity.

15. Proverbs 5:18-19 - "May your fountain be blessed, and may you rejoice in the wife of your youth... may you ever be intoxicated with her love."

Verses about marriage celebrating sexual pleasure, these encourage couples to maintain attraction and passion. Verses about marriage like this show that Scripture values ongoing sexual delight in marriage. These verses about marriage counter cultural narratives suggesting married sex becomes boring.

Verses About Marriage Purpose and Covenant

16. Genesis 2:18 - "The Lord God said, 'It is not good for the man to be alone. I will make a helper suitable for him.'"

Among verses about marriage addressing its purpose, this reveals companionship as primary. Verses about marriage like this show that humans are relational by design. These verses about marriage indicate that marriage fulfills deep needs for connection.

17. Malachi 2:14 - "You ask, 'Why?' It is because the Lord is acting as the witness between you and the wife of your youth, because you have broken faith with her, though she is your partner, the wife of your covenant."

Verses about marriage addressing covenant nature, this shows God witnesses marital promises. Verses about marriage like this treat infidelity as covenant-breaking before God Himself. These verses about marriage emphasize the sacred dimension of marital commitment.

18. Matthew 19:6 - "So they are no longer two, but one flesh. Therefore what God has joined together, let no one separate."

Jesus' verses about marriage affirm permanence. Verses about marriage featuring Christ's teaching emphasize that God actively joins couples together. These verses about marriage show that separation violates God's design.

19. Ephesians 5:31-32 - "For this reason a man will leave his father and mother and be united to his wife, and the two will become one flesh. This is a profound mystery—but I am talking about Christ and the church."

Verses about marriage connecting it to redemptive mystery, these show marriage mirrors Christ-church relationship. Verses about marriage like this reveal transcendent spiritual significance. These verses about marriage show that your marriage testifies to God's covenant love.

Verses About Marriage Challenges and Conflict

20. Ephesians 4:2-3 - "Be completely humble and gentle; be patient, bearing with one another in love. Make every effort to keep the unity of the Spirit through the bond of peace."

Verses about marriage addressing conflict, these call for active peacemaking. Verses about marriage like this show that maintaining unity requires intentional effort. These verses about marriage teach that humility and gentleness resolve conflict.

21. 1 Peter 3:8-9 - "Finally, all of you, be like-minded, be sympathetic, love one another, be compassionate and humble. Do not repay evil with evil or insult with insult. On the contrary, repay evil with good, and seek peace and pursue it."

Verses about marriage requiring graceful response to hurt, these teach redemptive reactions. Verses about marriage like this show that bitterness and retaliation destroy relationships. These verses about marriage emphasize pursuing peace actively.

22. Matthew 18:21-22 - "Then Peter came to Jesus and asked, 'Lord, how many times shall I forgive my brother or sister who sins against me? Up to seven times?' Jesus answered, 'I tell you, not seven times, but seventy-seven times.'"

Though verses about marriage broader than the marriage context, these apply directly. Verses about marriage emphasizing forgiveness show that endless forgiveness characterizes Christian relationships. These verses about marriage prevent grudges from festering into resentment.

Verses About Choosing a Spouse

23. 2 Corinthians 6:14 - "Do not be unequally yoked with unbelievers. For what do they have in common? Or what fellowship can light have with darkness?"

Critical verses about marriage addressing spouse selection, this demands spiritual compatibility. Verses about marriage like this show that shared faith is non-negotiable. These verses about marriage prevent marrying someone who doesn't share your deepest commitments.

24. Amos 3:3 - "Do two walk together unless they have agreed to do so?"

Verses about marriage addressing compatibility, this shows that agreement matters fundamentally. Verses about marriage like this suggest that couples must align on core values. These verses about marriage indicate that walking together requires shared direction.

25. Proverbs 27:12 - "The prudent see danger and take refuge, but the simple keep going and pay the penalty."

Verses about marriage addressing wisdom in relationships, this teaches that foresight matters. Verses about marriage like this encourage couples to address problems early. These verses about marriage suggest that ignoring warning signs leads to serious consequences.

FAQ

Q: Are there Bible verses about marriage for couples in crisis? A: Yes. Verses about marriage addressing challenges include Matthew 18:21-22 (forgiveness), Ephesians 4:2-3 (peacemaking), and 1 Peter 3:8-9 (grace). These verses about marriage encourage couples to pursue reconciliation rather than separation.

Q: What Bible verses about marriage are best for wedding ceremonies? A: Popular verses about marriage for weddings include Genesis 2:24, Ephesians 5:25-33, Song of Songs 4:9-11, and 1 Corinthians 13:4-5. These verses about marriage celebrate commitment, love, and unity.

Q: Are there Bible verses about marriage addressing singleness? A: Yes. While verses about marriage celebrate the relationship, 1 Corinthians 7 includes teaching that singleness is valid. These verses about marriage acknowledge that marriage isn't mandatory.

Q: What Bible verses about marriage address infidelity? A: Key verses about marriage include Proverbs 5 (marital fidelity), Malachi 2:14-16 (covenant breaking), and Hebrews 13:4 (sexual purity). These verses about marriage warn strongly against unfaithfulness.

Q: Are there encouraging Bible verses about marriage for struggling couples? A: Yes. Verses about marriage offering hope include Proverbs 27:12 (addressing problems early), 1 Peter 3:7 (understanding and honor), and Matthew 18:21-22 (forgiveness). These verses about marriage show that God supports couples through difficulty.

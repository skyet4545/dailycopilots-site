Frequently Asked Questions

Philippians 4:6-7 is a foundational verse: “Do not be anxious about anything, but in every situation, by prayer and petition, with thanksgiving, present your requests to God.” Also powerful are 1 Peter 5:7 — “Cast all your anxiety on him because he cares for you” — and John 14:27, where Jesus promises his peace directly.
Jeremiah 29:11 is among the most beloved hope verses: “For I know the plans I have for you, declares the Lord, plans to prosper you and not to harm you, plans to give you hope and a future.” Romans 15:13 and Lamentations 3:22-23 are also treasured passages of encouragement.
Philippians 4:13 is the most quoted: “I can do all this through him who gives me strength.” Isaiah 40:31 promises that those who hope in the Lord will renew their strength, and Psalm 46:1 declares God to be “our refuge and strength, an ever-present help in trouble.”
Psalm 34:18 offers direct comfort: “The Lord is close to the brokenhearted and saves those who are crushed in spirit.” Revelation 21:4 looks to eternal hope, while Matthew 5:4 — “Blessed are those who mourn, for they will be comforted” — shows that grief is acknowledged and honored by God.
1 Corinthians 13:4-7 provides the Bible’s most complete picture of love. John 3:16 captures God’s love for humanity, and Romans 8:38-39 is a powerful assurance that nothing in creation can separate us from God’s love in Christ Jesus.
Isaiah 41:10 speaks directly: “So do not fear, for I am with you.” Joshua 1:9 is a command to courage, and 2 Timothy 1:7 reminds us that God’s Spirit produces power, love, and self-discipline rather than fear. 1 John 4:18 also teaches that perfect love drives out fear.

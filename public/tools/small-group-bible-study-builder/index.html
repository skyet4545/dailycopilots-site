    What makes a strong Bible study discussion?

    A good group study keeps people in the passage long enough to notice what is actually there, then guides them toward faithful response. The leader's job is not to produce every answer, but to protect the text, draw out careful observation, and keep application concrete. If you mainly need prompts for a lesson, use the free Bible Study Questions Generator to create observation, interpretation, application, prayer, and follow-up questions.

    1. Let the group read the passage aloud before discussion.
    2. Ask observation questions before interpretation questions.
    3. Name difficult or commonly misused parts of the passage.
    4. Keep application specific, communal, and prayerful.

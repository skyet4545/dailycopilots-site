How to write Bible study questions that do not skip the text

A strong question does not merely ask people to share opinions. It slows the group down, points them to details in the passage, and then helps them respond faithfully.

  1. Observation: What does the text say?
  2. Interpretation: What does it mean in context?
  3. Theology: What does it show about God, humanity, sin, grace, or redemption?
  4. Application: What faithful response follows from the passage?
  5. Prayer: How should this shape what we ask God to do in us?

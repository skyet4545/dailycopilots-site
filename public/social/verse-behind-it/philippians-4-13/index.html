The Verse Behind It

Philippians 4:13 is not a promise that every ambition will succeed.

Paul writes, “I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me,” after describing seasons of plenty and seasons of need. The strength he names is Christ’s strength to remain faithful and content in either circumstance.

“I have learned in whatever situation I am to be content… I can do all things through him who strengthens me.”
Philippians 4:11–13

That does not make the verse smaller. It makes it steadier: Christ strengthens his people when circumstances are abundant, difficult, ordinary, or uncertain.

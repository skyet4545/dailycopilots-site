The Verse Behind It

Romans 8:28 does not minimize pain.

Paul writes this promise in a chapter that speaks candidly about weakness, suffering, and creation groaning. The verse should not be used to make another person’s pain small.

“In all things God works for the good of those who love him, who have been called according to his purpose.”
Romans 8:28

It does not say every event is good. It says suffering is not outside God’s care or final purpose for his people.

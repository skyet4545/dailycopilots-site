The Verse Behind It

Jeremiah 29:11 was written to people in exile.

The promise comes to people living far from home in a season they did not choose. In the same letter, God tells them to build, plant, pray, and seek the good of the city where they are living.

“For I know the plans I have for you… plans for welfare and not for evil, to give you a future and a hope.”
Jeremiah 29:11

This is not shallow optimism or an immediate escape route. It is a promise that exile is not abandonment and that God remains faithful through a long season.

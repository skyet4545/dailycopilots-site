The Verse Behind It

Does “judge not” end moral discernment?

Jesus’ warning in Matthew 7 confronts hypocrisy and self-righteousness: the person preoccupied with another person’s speck while ignoring a log in their own eye.

“Judge not, that you be not judged… first take the log out of your own eye.”
Matthew 7:1–5

The same teaching later calls followers to recognize false prophets by their fruit. Jesus is not telling us to abandon wisdom; he is warning against proud, condemning judgment.

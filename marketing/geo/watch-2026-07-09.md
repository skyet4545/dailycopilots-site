## Audit

**46 unique pages reviewed** (52 rows in `done_topics.txt` dated 2026-07-02 → 2026-07-09; 6 duplicate slugs). **2 real issues found, both fixed and deployed.**

### Issue 1 — Fabricated competitor pricing tier (accuracy, high severity)

`best-bible-app-for-sermon-prep` claimed Logos offers a **"Logos Plus" tier at $4.99/month**, in two places (a comparison-table cell and body prose). **No such tier exists.** Verified against two Logos-owned pages ([logos.com/configure/subscriptions](https://www.logos.com/configure/subscriptions), [logos.com/subscription-faq](https://www.logos.com/subscription-faq)): the tiers are Premium ($9.99/mo, $99.99/yr), Pro ($14.99/mo, $149.99/yr), and Max ($19.99/mo, $199.99/yr).

- **Fixed:** removed the invented tier from the table; replaced the prose claim with the verified annual figures.
- This is the exact failure mode the audit exists to catch — a confident, specific, false price about a named competitor sitting on a page whose whole purpose is to be trusted on competitor pricing.

### Issue 2 — Dangling quotation mark in 32 page titles (quality, medium severity)

32 of 46 pages shipped with a truncated title: `What Does Psalm 30:5 Mean? "Joy Comes in the Morning` — opening quote, no closing quote. It appeared in **7 places per page**: `<title>`, `<h1>`, `og:title`, `twitter:title`, the visible breadcrumb, the JSON-LD `Article.headline`, and the JSON-LD `BreadcrumbList` name — plus the card on `/blog/` and the title column in `done_topics.txt`.

Because `<title>` is the search-result headline and the JSON-LD headline is what AI answer engines read, this was actively degrading the surface this very report measures.

- **Fixed:** appended the closing quote across all 7 fields on 32 pages, 32 index cards, and 32 `done_topics.txt` rows. All JSON-LD blocks re-validated as parseable; 0 unbalanced titles remain.

**Root cause (NOT fixed — outside this task's write scope):** [`blog-engine/publish-blog-drafts.js:36`](../../blog-engine/publish-blog-drafts.js)

```js
meta[kv[1]] = kv[2].replace(/^["']|["']$/g, '').trim();
```

The regex strips a leading **or** trailing quote independently. It is meant to unwrap YAML quoting (`title: "Foo"`), but any title *ending* in a quoted phrase loses its closing quote and keeps the interior opening one. Suggested fix: only strip when both ends are matching quotes, e.g.

```js
const v = kv[2].trim();
meta[kv[1]] = /^(["']).*\1$/.test(v) ? v.slice(1, -1) : v;
```

**Until this is patched, every future verse page whose title ends in a quoted phrase ships broken.** Already-published slugs are safe (the publisher skips slugs recorded in `done_topics.txt`).

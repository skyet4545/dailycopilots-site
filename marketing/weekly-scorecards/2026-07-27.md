## What won / what flopped / what changes

**What won:** Myths remain the franchise — 6 of the all-time top 10, all-time median 207 (1.4× verse, 1.6× answer) and mean 257 (~2× both). The specific winning shape is a *familiar phrase or story stated in the viewer's own words, then reversed* ("follow your heart", "a fool and his money", "practice what you preach", the Christmas innkeeper).

**What flopped:** Views fell off a cliff from Jul 23 — median 202 → 47 WoW, and Jul 23–26 videos are stuck in single/double digits at ages where earlier videos had already cleared 130+. Jul 22 was a total outage: zero posts on YouTube *and* Facebook. The weakest myths were obscure-detail corrections ("Delilah cut Samson's hair" = 4 views, "Angels have wings and halos" = 11) rather than familiar-phrase reversals.

**What changes:** Sharpened one hook example in `prompts/myth.md` to make the winning familiar-phrase-reversal pattern explicit and to name the losing pattern by example. Pool order left untouched — see below.

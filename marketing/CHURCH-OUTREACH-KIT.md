# Church & Ministry Outreach Kit v1 (2026-07-04)

Why: Hallow's core growth loop was institutional — parishes, schools, ministries — not ads.
Our micro version: youth pastors, small-church staff, homeschool co-ops, church social admins.
One warm church = dozens of installs with HIGH retention (D7 retention is now an App Store
ranking input, so these installs also lift organic rank).

Free **Pro offer codes** for church staff/small-group leaders (ASC → Subscriptions →
Offer Codes → one-time codes; e.g. 3-month free Pro, batch of 25 per church).

# Bible Copilot — Social-to-Study System

## Production contract

**Series:** The Verse Behind It  
**Format:** 30–60 second faceless vertical video, 9:16, captions required.  
**Voice:** Warm, precise, reverent; never sensational or denominator-specific on contested doctrine.  
**CTA:** "Study this passage in Bible Copilot."

## First campaign

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Campaign | `bible_verse_behind_it` |
| Creative | `bcv_001` |
| Topic | `philippians_4_13` |
| Landing page | `/social/verse-behind-it/philippians-4-13/` |
| Recommended app mode | Interpret |
| Primary metric | `first_question_completed` from social acquisition |

## Script template

1. State the commonly held interpretation or real question.
2. Read or cite the immediate context.
3. Give one concise, defensible explanation.
4. Invite the viewer to study the whole passage—not merely affirm the clip.

## Required review

- Scripture reference and surrounding-context claim verified.
- No claims of certainty on contested doctrine.
- No fear, urgency, or outcome-guarantee language.
- CTA source uses `utm_source`, `utm_medium=organic_social`, `utm_campaign=bible_verse_behind_it`, and `utm_content=bcv_001`.

## Scorecard fields

`creative_id`, `topic_id`, views, saves, shares, profile visits, social landing views, App Store handoffs, first completed questions, trials, paid starts, retained paid users, contribution margin.

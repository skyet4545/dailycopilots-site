## Measurement protocol

Record the same page rows after 7, 14, 21, and 28 days. The 28-day decision rule is:

- Keep when CTR improves by at least 30% without a material position decline.
- Investigate content/ranking when position falls by more than two places.
- Test a second title only when impressions are sufficient and the first treatment does not improve CTR.
- Never change the URL during a title test.

Primary target: move the selected-page weighted CTR above 2%. Site target: move overall CTR from 0.9% toward 2% while expanding impressions.

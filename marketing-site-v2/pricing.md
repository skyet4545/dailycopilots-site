## Pricing Tiers

### Free Tier

Q: What does the free tier include?
A: The free tier includes 3 questions per day across any of the six study modes, access to the Gospel of John reading plan, no ads, and no credit card required. There is no time limit on the free tier.

Q: Is the free tier enough for daily devotional use?
A: Yes. 3 questions per day is designed to cover a standard morning devotional session — one passage studied through one or two modes.

---

### Pro Monthly

Q: What does Pro Monthly cost?
A: $9.99 per month, billed monthly through the App Store.

Q: What does Pro Monthly include?
A: Unlimited questions per day across all six study modes (Summary, Observe, Interpret, Theology, Apply, Apologetics), all five reading plans (Gospel of John, Psalms, Romans, Genesis, Sermon on the Mount), full personal study journal with history and inline cross-references, and no ads.

Q: Does Pro Monthly include a free trial?
A: No. The 1-week free trial applies only to the annual plan.

Q: How do I cancel Pro Monthly?
A: Manage and cancel in Apple Settings: Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions > Bible Copilot. No support email required.

---

### Pro Annual

Q: What does Pro Annual cost?
A: $49.99 per year — equivalent to approximately $4.17 per month. This is roughly 58% savings compared to the monthly plan billed annually.

Q: What does Pro Annual include?
A: All Pro Monthly features (unlimited questions, all six modes, all five reading plans, full journal, no ads) plus a 1-week free trial.

Q: How does the 1-week free trial work?
A: The free trial applies to the annual plan only. Your Apple ID is not charged until day 8 of the trial. You can cancel any time before day 8 through Apple Settings and will not be charged.

Q: How do I cancel Pro Annual or the free trial?
A: Manage in Apple Settings: Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions > Bible Copilot. Cancel before the trial ends to avoid being charged. Apple handles all billing — there is no separate account to log into or cancel.

## Important Notes

- All billing is handled by Apple through the App Store. Republic Publishing LLC does not store payment information.
- Subscriptions auto-renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
- Pricing is in USD. App Store pricing may vary by region.
- To cancel, always use Apple Settings — not an email to support.
- Bible Copilot does not use your study questions or journal entries to train AI models.

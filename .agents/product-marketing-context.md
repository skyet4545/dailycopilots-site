# Bible Copilot Product Context

Last updated: 2026-08-03  
Authority: `product-truth.json` is the current source contract. Canonical public website: `https://mybiblecopilot.com`.

## Product overview

**One-liner:** Bible Copilot is the everyday Christian's verse-anchored study companion, helping a reader move from a Bible question to a careful, Scripture-cited study of the passage.

It serves ordinary Christians, returning readers, parents, small-group leaders, apologetics learners, and students. It is not a pastor, a denomination, a substitute for Scripture, trusted scholarship, local-church discipleship, or professional care.

## Formation loop

1. Read a passage or name a real Bible question.
2. Choose a study lens or accept an optional suggested pathway.
3. Observe the passage and its context.
4. Interpret with Scripture citations and cross-references.
5. Consider theology and application with denominational humility.
6. Save, journal, share, join a reading plan, or return for another study session.

## Study-mode decision

The six modes—Summary, Observe, Interpret, Theology, Apply, and Apologetics—remain available as direct reader choices. The product may offer a gentle, optional pathway for newcomers: Summary → Observe → Interpret → Apply, with Theology and Apologetics surfaced when the question warrants them. The pathway is a recommendation, never a lock, spiritual test, or claim that one method fits every Christian tradition.

## Core promises and boundaries

- Approved public claims come only from `product-truth.json`: verse-anchored study, Scripture-cited explanations, three free questions daily, no account required, and six guided study modes.
- Preserve the canonical Pro offer in that contract: $9.99 monthly and $49.99 annually, each with a seven-day trial.
- Present major Christian interpretive positions fairly where traditions differ; do not use the app to adjudicate a single denominational position as unquestionable.
- Cite Scripture precisely, identify uncertainty, and encourage verification through Scripture, trusted commentaries, and pastoral guidance.
- Do not claim seminary-grade, scholarly-level, first-in-category, or outcome-guaranteed authority without fresh substantiation.
- For self-harm, abuse, medical, mental-health, or imminent-safety situations, do not let Bible study substitute for immediate qualified or emergency support.

## Brand voice

Warm, precise, reverent, and non-preachy. Speak as a trustworthy study companion: neither a vague devotional feed nor an academic performance tool.

## Goals

The first meaningful outcome is a completed, trustworthy study session. The durable outcome is a reader who returns to Scripture with greater clarity and a healthier habit of study. Acquisition, onboarding, trial, paid conversion, and retention claims must stay tied to verified product-specific evidence.
